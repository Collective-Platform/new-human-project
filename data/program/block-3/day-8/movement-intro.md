## MOVEMENT 2 — The Spirit and Character

# What the Spirit most wants to grow in you

Days 8–14 · Galatians 5 · Romans 8 · 2 Corinthians 3

---

If Movement One was about recovering who the Spirit is, Movement Two is about what he most wants to do in you. Not primarily what gifts he wants to give, but what character he wants to form. The fruit of the Spirit is not a reward for the spiritually advanced: it is the natural growth of a life surrendered to his presence. These seven days take each aspect of the fruit seriously, and ask what it looks like in a real congregation, in real relationships, under real pressure.
