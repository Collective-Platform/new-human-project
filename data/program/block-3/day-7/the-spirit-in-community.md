## Today's Focus

The Spirit does not just live in individuals. He makes the whole community his home.

## Reading Notes

Do you not know that you (plural) are the temple of the Holy Spirit? The dominant Western and charismatic understanding of this verse has been intensely individual: my body is a temple. But the Greek is unambiguous, the you is plural.

The building Paul describes is the whole community of believers, fitted together, growing into a holy temple in the Lord. The Spirit does not merely live in scattered individuals who happen to gather on Sundays. He inhabits the new humanity as a whole: the relationships, the gathered worship, the shared meals, the acts of service, the reconciled conflicts.

This is one of the most important truths about the Spirit: he is building something communal. He is not just filling individual lives. He is constructing a dwelling place, a community that together displays the presence of God to the world.

## Key Idea

The Spirit's primary home is not the individual soul. It is the community of the new humanity and he is building it, fitting each person together.

## Reflection

How does knowing the Spirit lives in your community (not just in you personally) change the way you think about your relationships with other believers?

## Today's Practice

This week, instead of only asking God to fill you personally, pray for the Spirit's presence in your church as a whole. Name three specific relationships in your community and pray the Spirit would work through them.

What the Spirit most wants to grow in you

Galatians 5 · Romans 8 · 2 Corinthians 3

If Movement One was about recovering who the Spirit is, Movement Two is about what he most wants to do in you. Not primarily what gifts he wants to give, but what character he wants to form. The fruit of the Spirit is not a reward for the spiritually advanced: it is the natural growth of a life surrendered to his presence. These seven days take each aspect of the fruit seriously, and ask what it looks like in a real congregation, in real relationships, under real pressure.
