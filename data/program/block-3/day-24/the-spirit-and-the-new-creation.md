## Today's Focus

The Spirit is not just transforming individuals. He is the firstfruits of a renewed world.

## Reading Notes

We have the firstfruits of the Spirit. In the language of harvest, firstfruits are the first portion that guarantees the rest of the harvest is coming. The Spirit in us is not just about our personal spiritual experience, he is the down payment on the renewal of everything. He is the foretaste of the new creation.

Ezekiel's vision is breathtaking: I will give you a new heart and put a new spirit in you.

God is not repairing fallen humanity. He is creating something new from the inside out. And what God is doing in the Spirit-filled community now is a sign and instrument of what he will do for all of creation.

The new humanity is not just good news for the people in the building on Sunday. It is the beginning of the world's restoration.

## Key Idea

The Spirit in you is the firstfruits, the guarantee that everything is being made new. You are not just being renewed. You are a sign of the world's renewal.

## Reflection

How does the vision of new creation change the way you think about the Spirit's work in your community? What would it look like for your church to be a visible foretaste of renewal in your local area?

## Today's Practice

As a community: identify one place in your local area where the world is broken. Pray specifically for the Spirit to move there. Then ask: is there one step our community could take to be part of that renewal?
