## Today's Focus

The gifts of the Spirit are not personal spiritual achievements. They are tools for building the community.

## Reading Notes

To each is given the manifestation of the Spirit for the common good. These six words reframe everything about spiritual gifts. They are not given to make any individual impressive. They are not evidence of a person’s spiritual level. They are not the measure of closeness to God. They are given for the common good, for the benefit of the whole community.

The word manifestation suggests visibility: the gifts make the Spirit's presence visible in the community. Someone speaks a word of wisdom and the community is helped. Someone exercises discernment and a danger is avoided. Someone brings healing and a broken person is restored.

The gifts are the Spirit's fingerprints on the community he is building. They are beautiful and needed but they are servants, not masters. They serve the community; the community does not exist to display them.

## Key Idea

Spiritual gifts are not personal trophies. They are tools the Spirit gives to specific people to build the whole community.

## Reflection

How have spiritual gifts functioned in your community, as markers of status or as tools of service? What would a gift-culture oriented entirely around the common good look like in your community?

## Today's Practice

Think about a gift or ability you have, spiritual or natural. Ask: who in my community could I serve with this this week? Do it quietly, without drawing attention to yourself.
