## MOVEMENT 4 — The Spirit and Community

# What the Spirit is building and the gifts he uses to build it

Days 21–25 · 1 Corinthians 12–14 · Acts 2 · Romans 12

---

We arrive finally at the gifts — not because they are unimportant, but because they can only be understood rightly from where we now stand. The gifts of the Spirit are not the summit of spiritual life. They are tools given by the sovereign Spirit to build the community he is forming. These final five days place the gifts in their proper context: not as marks of spiritual superiority, but as acts of service in the new humanity. And they end with the vision of what the Spirit is building — a community that displays the character of Christ to the world.
