## Today's Focus

The Spirit calls us to maturity, which includes testing what claims to be from him.

## Reading Notes

Do not quench the Spirit. Do not despise prophecies. But test everything. Hold fast to what is good. Paul holds together what we tend to separate: openness and discernment. He does not say 'accept everything' or 'reject everything', he says test. John says the same: do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits.

This is not scepticism. It is maturity. A community that accepts everything claimed in the Spirit's name is not more spiritual, it is more vulnerable. The gifts need the community's collective discernment. When someone brings a prophetic word or spiritual impression, the community should weigh it: Does it glorify Christ? Does it accord with Scripture? Does it build the community up? Does it bear the Spirit's character of love and peace?

Maturity in the gifts is not having more of them. It is knowing how to handle them wisely.

## Key Idea

Testing spiritual experience is not unbelief, it is maturity. The Spirit who gives the gifts also gives discernment to weigh them.

## Reflection

How does your church currently handle the testing and weighing of prophecy or spiritual experiences? Is there a healthy culture of communal discernment?

## Today's Practice

As a group or personally: think of a spiritual experience or claim you have encountered recently. Walk through the questions: Does it glorify Christ? Does it accord with Scripture? Does it bear the Spirit's fruit? What do you conclude?
