## Today's Focus

Being filled with the Spirit is not a one-time event. It is a daily, ongoing way of life.

## Reading Notes

Keep on walking by the Spirit. Be continually filled with the Spirit. Both verbs in the original Greek are present tense, ongoing, not a single moment of filling but a continuous walking, a daily being filled.

The command to be filled with the Spirit is not addressed to a spiritual elite, it is addressed to every ordinary believer in Ephesus: the butchers, the sailors, the housewives, the slaves. And it is a command, not a suggestion.

The implication is that we can resist being filled, that we can walk in the flesh rather than the Spirit, that daily surrender is required.

The Spirit-filled life is not a summit you reach, it is a path you walk, daily, in deliberate, moment-by-moment cooperation.

## Key Idea

Being filled with the Spirit is not a badge you earn once. It is a daily surrender, a continuous choosing to walk in him rather than in yourself.

## Reflection

What does the beginning of your day look like? Is there a moment of deliberate surrender to the Spirit or does the day begin without him?

## Today's Practice

For the rest of this study, begin each morning with a simple act of surrender: 'Spirit, I am not walking today without you. Fill me afresh.' Make it as natural as putting on your shoes.
