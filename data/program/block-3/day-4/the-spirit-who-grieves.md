## Today's Focus

A person can be grieved. That tells us everything about the kind of relationship God wants with us.

## Reading Notes

Do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God.

This is one of the most revealing verses in Scripture about the Spirit's personhood. You cannot grieve an electricity supply. You cannot offend a force. Grief is the response of a person who loves, who is in relationship, who has expectations rooted in care.

The Spirit grieves when believers lie, when anger turns to malice, when bitterness takes root, when sexual immorality is embraced, when the community fractures. He grieves not because rules have been broken but because the relationship he longs for has been damaged.

This verse calls for a deep reorientation: the most important question about the Holy Spirit is not 'what can he do?' but 'how is my relationship with him?'

## Key Idea

The fact that the Spirit can be grieved is the most personal thing Paul ever says about him. It means your choices affect a relationship not just a spiritual outcome.

## Reflection

Is there anything in your life right now that you sense is grieving the Holy Spirit? Not from guilt but from an honest awareness of the relationship?

## Today's Practice

Pray through Ephesians 4:25-32 as a personal inventory. For each behaviour Paul names, ask the Spirit honestly: 'Is this present in my life?' Listen without defending yourself.
