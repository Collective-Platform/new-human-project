## Today's Focus

Your physical body matters to the Spirit: he does not want to escape it.

## Reading Notes

If the Spirit of him who raised Jesus from the dead dwells in you, he who raised Christ Jesus from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies through his Spirit who dwells in you.

The Spirit is not trying to help you escape your body. He is transforming it, inhabiting it, and will ultimately glorify it. This is the basis for Paul's appeal: your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit. Therefore honour God with your body. What you eat, how you sleep, how you treat your sexuality, how you rest and work, all of this is spiritual.

The division between 'spiritual life' and physical life is not a biblical one. The Spirit who hovered over creation at the beginning is interested in every cell of the body he now inhabits. The new humanity does not have a private 'spiritual' life and a separate physical life. They are one.

## Key Idea

The Spirit does not want to escape your body. He wants to inhabit and transform it, which means your physical life is your spiritual life.

## Reflection

How do you think about the relationship between your body and your spiritual life? Are there physical habits (rest, eating, exercise, sexuality) that you have never considered as spiritual matters?

## Today's Practice

Choose one physical habit and bring it deliberately under the Spirit's lordship this week. It might be sleep, what you eat, what you watch, how you exercise. Ask the Spirit to be present in that specific area.
