## Today's Focus

The Spirit distributes gifts differently on purpose because the community needs every part, not just the visible ones.

## Reading Notes

The body does not consist of one member but of many. Paul's anatomy of the church is both theologically precise and deeply pastoral. The eye cannot say to the hand, 'I have no need of you.' The more visible members cannot look down on the less visible ones. And the less visible cannot excuse themselves from belonging: I am not an eye, so I do not belong to the body.

The Spirit distributes gifts sovereignly as he wills, which means your gift is not your achievement and someone else's different gift is not a sign of their inferior spirituality. The person with an obscure, unsung gift is as essential as the person with the platform gift.

The new humanity is built not by the accumulation of impressive individuals but by every member functioning in their Spirit-given role, including the ones the congregation rarely thanks.

## Key Idea

The Spirit gives different gifts on purpose, so the community will need each other. There is no gift so small it doesn't matter, and no gift so large it can stand alone.

## Reflection

Have you ever felt that your contribution was too small to matter? Or have you ever undervalued someone else's contribution? What does this passage say to both?

## Today's Practice

Identify one person in your church whose contribution is rarely noticed or celebrated. Find a specific way to tell them this week that their gift matters and means something to you personally.
