## Today's Focus

The Spirit's presence in suffering is not escape from it: it is companionship through it.

## Reading Notes

We suffer with him in order that we may also be glorified with him. The Spirit in us does not promise us immunity from suffering, he promises us that suffering is not the last word. Paul sets the present sufferings in the context of future glory, and says the whole creation is groaning, not just us.

We groan inwardly as we wait for adoption, for the redemption of our bodies. There is an honest realism here: the Spirit does not always remove the pain. Sometimes he groans with us in it.

The healing does not always come in this life. The breakthrough does not always arrive on our schedule. And yet the Spirit is present. He is interceding. He is transforming. The new humanity lives in the tension of the already and the not yet, and the Spirit holds us in that tension with hope.

## Key Idea

The Spirit does not always remove the suffering. He groans with us in it and holds us toward the glory that is coming.

## Reflection

Is there suffering in your life that you have been expecting God to remove? What would it mean to receive the Spirit's companionship within it, rather than waiting for its removal?

## Today's Practice

Write an honest prayer about one area of ongoing pain or disappointment in your life. Don't ask for it to be removed. Ask the Spirit to be present in it and for the hope of glory to become more real than the suffering.
