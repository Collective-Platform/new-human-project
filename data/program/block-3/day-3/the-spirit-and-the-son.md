## Today's Focus

To understand the Holy Spirit, we must see how he relates to Jesus: inseparably.

## Reading Notes

The Holy Spirit descended on Jesus at his baptism like a dove. Jesus was anointed with the Spirit without measure. He was led by the Spirit into the wilderness, returned in the power of the Spirit to Galilee, and proclaimed that the Spirit of the Lord was upon him to bring good news to the poor.

The Spirit's primary ministry throughout history has been to bring people into relationship with Jesus and to form Christ's character in believers. This is the Spirit's defining agenda: not to draw attention to himself, but to glorify Christ.

Any experience that claims to be from the Spirit but draws attention away from Jesus has lost its compass. The Spirit and the Son are inseparable, you cannot have one without the other.

## Key Idea

The Spirit's deepest desire is not to put himself on display. It is to make Jesus real in you and through you.

## Reflection

In your church's experience of the Holy Spirit, has Jesus been the centre or have the experiences themselves become the focus? How do you tell the difference?

## Today's Practice

Read John 16:12-15 slowly. Then ask the Holy Spirit to show you something true about Jesus today that you haven't fully seen before. Wait, and write down what comes.
