## MOVEMENT 3 — The Spirit and Daily Life

# Walking in the Spirit from Monday to Saturday

Days 15–20 · Romans 8 · Galatians 5 · Ezekiel 36

---

The Spirit is not confined to Sunday. He does not live in the church building and visit you occasionally. He lives in you — in the middle of the commute, the difficult conversation, the exhaustion, the temptation, the ordinary Tuesday. These six days are about what it actually looks like to walk in the Spirit in the rhythms of real life: prayer, decision-making, suffering, temptation, work, and the body.
