## Today's Focus

What do you do when you don't know how to pray? The Spirit does.

## Reading Notes

The Spirit helps us in our weakness. For we do not know what to pray for as we ought, but the Spirit himself intercedes for us with groanings too deep for words. This is one of the most intimate and relieving verses in the New Testament.

The Spirit is not waiting for you to produce the right words, the right feelings, the right level of faith. He is already interceding within you, groaning with and for you in the depths of your situation.

You are never alone in prayer, even when you feel most alone. The Spirit is already there, interceding with a fluency and faithfulness that no human expression can match. And because he knows the mind of God, he intercedes according to the will of God, which means your wordless, broken, exhausted prayer is not wasted. It is held.

## Key Idea

You are never alone in prayer, even when you have no words. The Spirit is already interceding within you, deeper than language.

## Reflection

When do you find prayer hardest? Knowing that in that specific difficulty, that the Spirit is already interceding within you, how do you feel?

## Today's Practice

Today, set aside ten minutes for prayer where you give yourself permission to have no words. Simply sit. Tell the Spirit: 'I have nothing today, intercede for me.' Then rest in that.
