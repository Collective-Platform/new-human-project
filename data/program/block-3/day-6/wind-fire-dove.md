## Today's Focus

The images Scripture uses for the Spirit tell us something about his character that propositions alone cannot.

## Reading Notes

The Spirit comes as a dove: gentle, resting only where there is peace. He comes as wind: invisible, sovereign, impossible to control, going where he wills. He comes as fire: purifying, consuming what cannot last, illuminating the darkness. He comes as water: life-giving, flowing, satisfying the deepest thirst.

These images are not decorative. They are revelatory. The dove tells us the Spirit is not coerced. The wind tells us he is sovereign. The fire tells us he is holy and purifying. The water tells us he is the source of life.

Together they reveal a Spirit who is far greater and far more personal than any single image can capture, one who comes on his own terms, in his own way, to those who truly welcome him.

## Key Idea

You cannot manufacture the Spirit's presence. Like a dove, he rests where he finds welcome – in the humble and the surrendered.

## Reflection

Which image of the Spirit (dove, wind, fire or water) speaks most to where you are right now? What does it tell you about what you need from him?

## Today's Practice

Spend ten minutes in complete silence today. No music, no speaking. Simply wait. Invite the Spirit to come as he chooses. This is not a technique, it is an act of surrender.
