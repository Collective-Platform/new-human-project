## Today's Focus

The Spirit produces a joy that circumstances cannot give and cannot take away.

## Reading Notes

The kingdom of God is not food and drink but righteousness and peace and joy in the Holy Spirit. Joy is not a personality trait. It is a fruit of the Spirit which means it is available to the melancholy, the grieving, the anxious, and the exhausted.

The Thessalonians received the word in much affliction, with the joy of the Holy Spirit. Joy and affliction together. Not despite the suffering but in it. This is the distinction the New Testament consistently draws: happiness depends on circumstances going well, but joy is the orientation of a heart that has found its home in God.

It is possible to be deeply joyful in a painful season not because you are pretending the pain isn't there, but because the Spirit is more present than the pain.

## Key Idea

Joy in the Spirit is not the absence of pain. It is about your inner self being anchored by the Spirit's presence while you navigate external circumstances.

## Reflection

Is there a distinction in your experience between happiness and the deeper joy Paul describes? When have you experienced joy in a difficult season?

## Today's Practice

Write down three things that are difficult in your life right now. Then write, next to each one, one true thing about God that the difficulty does not change. Read both lists to him in prayer.
