## Today's Focus

Character formation is not an achievement: it is a gradual transformation the Spirit does in us as we behold Christ.

## Reading Notes

We all, with unveiled face, beholding the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another. For this comes from the Lord who is the Spirit.

This is one of the most important verses in the New Testament about how Christian formation actually works. Not through dramatic interventions alone, but through a slow, continuous transformation from glory to glory, one degree at a time. And the mechanism is beholding: you become like what you look at.

The Spirit transforms us as we fix our gaze on Jesus in Scripture, in prayer, in worship, in community. The spiritual disciplines matter not as achievements that earn favour, but as the conditions in which beholding happens. The Spirit does the transforming. Our part is simply to keep turning our face toward Christ.

## Key Idea

The Spirit transforms us as we behold Christ. You become like what you look at and the Spirit takes care of the transformation as you keep looking.

## Reflection

What practices in your life most help you 'behold' Christ, to actually look at him with sustained attention? What gets in the way of that most often?

## Today's Practice

For the remaining days of this study, begin each day with five minutes of what might be called 'beholding', read one short passage about Jesus slowly, not to learn information, but simply to look at him. Notice what the Spirit does.

The Spirit and Daily Life

Walking in the Spirit from Monday to Saturday

Romans 8 · Galatians 5 · Ezekiel 36

The Spirit is not confined to Sunday. He does not live in the church building and visit you occasionally. He lives in you, in the middle of the commute, the difficult conversation, the exhaustion, the temptation, the ordinary Tuesday. These six days are about what it actually looks like to walk in the Spirit in the rhythms of real life: prayer, decision-making, suffering, temptation, work, and the body.
