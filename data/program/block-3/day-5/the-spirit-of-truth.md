## Today's Focus

The Spirit does not replace our minds, he renews and guides them into truth.

## Reading Notes

When the Spirit of truth comes, he will guide you into all truth. This is not a promise that the Spirit will bypass your thinking and give you spiritual impressions that replace careful reflection. It is a promise of ongoing, relational guidance, the Spirit leading the disciples deeper into the truth that is already centred on Jesus.

The Spirit and the Word of God work together, not in opposition. He illuminates Scripture. He brings the written word to life in the specific situation. He does not contradict what has been revealed.

The Spirit of truth does not take us away from the text, he takes us deeper into it, opening our eyes to what was always there.

## Key Idea

The Spirit of truth leads you deeper into Jesus: who is the truth. He never leads you away from Scripture; he leads you into its depths.

## Reflection

Have you ever experienced a conflict between a spiritual impression and what Scripture teaches? How did you navigate it? What does today's passage offer for that situation?

## Today's Practice

Choose one passage of Scripture you find difficult or confusing. Before studying it, explicitly ask the Spirit of truth to guide your understanding. Study it. Notice what opens up.
