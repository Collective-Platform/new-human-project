## A Word Before You Begin

In Ephesians, we saw the blueprint. The new humanity — chosen, reconciled, built together as God's temple in the world. In Philippians, we watched that vision tested by real life: joy from a prison cell, humility in the face of conflict, peace that passes understanding, generosity that overflows from grace. Now we arrive at the question that underlies both: who is actually doing all this?

The answer is the Holy Spirit. He is the one who seals us into Christ (Ephesians 1:13). He is the one who strengthens the inner being (Ephesians 3:16). He is the one who fills the community (Ephesians 5:18). He is the one who produces the joy of Philippians in a prison cell and the peace that guards the heart. Without the Spirit, the New Human Project is an impressive vision with no engine.

These 25 days are an invitation to know the Spirit more fully: as a person, not merely a power; as a character-former, not merely a gift-giver; as the one who meets you on Tuesday morning, not just on Sunday; and as the architect of a community that will one day display the fullness of Christ to the world.

The goal is not to evaluate or compare different traditions of Christian experience. It is simply to let Scripture speak, to follow the biblical text wherever it leads, and to allow the full picture of the Spirit that the Bible paints to take root in our lives. The Spirit is far larger, far more personal, and far more present than any single category can capture.

Come, Holy Spirit. Not to perform for us, but to form us.
