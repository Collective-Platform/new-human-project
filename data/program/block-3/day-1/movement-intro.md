## MOVEMENT 1 — The Person of the Spirit

# Who is the Holy Spirit and why does it matter?

Days 1–7 · John 14–16 · Genesis 1–2 · Acts 2

---

Before we can speak honestly about the Holy Spirit's work, we need to recover something the church has always known but can easily forget: the Holy Spirit is a person, not a power. He is the third member of the Trinity — not a force to be directed, not an experience to be sought, but a divine person to be known. These seven days lay the only foundation worth building on.
