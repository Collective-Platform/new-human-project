## Today's Focus

Why does it matter whether the Holy Spirit is a person or a power?

## Reading Notes

Jesus does not promise his disciples an experience, an energy, or an upgrade. He promises them a Person (the Paraclete), the one called alongside. The word is deeply relational: a counsellor, advocate, comforter who comes to be with you and in you.

Notice the pronouns: Jesus says 'he', not 'it'. The Spirit knows, speaks, guides, grieves, and intercedes. He is not a spiritual electricity that flows when you press the right buttons. He is a divine person who chooses to make his home in human hearts.

This is the most important starting point for understanding everything that follows: you cannot channel a person. You can only know one.

## Key Idea

The Holy Spirit is not a power you access. He is a person who knows you and invites you into a relationship.

## Reflection

In your own experience, have you related to the Holy Spirit more as a power to be received or as a person to be known? What difference would it make to shift that?

## Today's Practice

Today, instead of asking God to 'send his Spirit' or 'pour out his power', try speaking to the Holy Spirit directly as a person. Introduce yourself, honestly. Tell him where you are.
