## Today's Focus

The final three fruits of the Spirit are grown in private, displayed in ordinary moments, and are marks of deep maturity.

## Reading Notes

Faithfulness (pistis) is reliability, steadiness over time. It is the fruit of being the same person in private that you are in public; the same on Tuesday morning as you were in Sunday's worship. It is one of the rarest qualities in any community.

Gentleness (prautes) is not weakness. It was used to described a tamed horse: enormous power under control, strength that does not need to prove itself. Jesus said he was gentle and lowly in heart and invited the weary to come to him.

Self-control (enkrateia) is mastery over the self: not repression, but the freedom of a person whose passions serve their purpose rather than rule them.

Together these three fruits are a portrait of maturity. They are grown in the ordinary, the hidden, the unspectacular. Nobody sees your faithfulness at 6am when no one is watching. But the Spirit does.

## Key Idea

Faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control are the fruits of maturity. They grow in private, show in ordinary moments, and they are the Spirit's truest work.

## Reflection

Which of these three: faithfulness, gentleness, self-control, do you most need the Spirit to grow in you right now? What is one concrete area of your life where it would show?

## Today's Practice

Pick one private practice this week that no one will see: keeping a commitment to yourself, controlling a habitual response, or responding gently to a provocation. Do it as an act of cooperation with the Spirit.
