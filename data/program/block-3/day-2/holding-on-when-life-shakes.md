**Hebrews 12:26-29**

26 At that time his voice shook the earth, but now he has promised, “Once more I will shake not only the earth but also the heavens.”[a] 27 The words “once more” indicate the removing of what can be shaken—that is, created things—so that what cannot be shaken may remain. 28 Therefore, since we are receiving a kingdom that cannot be shaken, let us be thankful, and so worship God acceptably with reverence and awe, 29 for our “God is a consuming fire.”

## Reading Notes

Every one of us longs for stability.

We want relationships that last, work that feels secure, finances we can depend on, good health, and a future we can plan for. There is nothing wrong with desiring these things. They are gifts from God that bring joy and purpose to our lives.

The challenge comes when we unknowingly build our security upon them. When life is going well, it is easy to believe that everything is under control. We feel confident because our circumstances are favourable. But life has a way of reminding us that nothing in this world is guaranteed.

A phone call can change everything. A doctor's report can alter the course of your future. A relationship can suddenly fall apart. A job you thought was secure can disappear.

When life begins to shake, it does more than disrupt our circumstances—it reveals where we have been placing our confidence.

The writer of Hebrews reminds believers that there are things which can be shaken and things which cannot. Kingdoms rise and fall. Wealth comes and goes. Success fades. Seasons change. Even our emotions can fluctuate from one day to the next.

But God's Kingdom remains. His character does not change. His promises do not fail. His love does not fluctuate with our performance. His presence does not leave us when life becomes difficult.

This is why emotional resilience is not built by trying to control every circumstance. It is built by learning to anchor your life in the One who never changes.

## Practice

Write down three things that you often depend on for stability—perhaps your career, finances, relationships, health, or personal achievements.

## Reflection

Where have I been looking for security apart from God?
