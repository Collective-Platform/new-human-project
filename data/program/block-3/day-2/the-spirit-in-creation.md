## Today's Focus

The Holy Spirit is not a New Testament arrival. He has been at work since before time began.

## Reading Notes

The Spirit of God was hovering over the waters before a single word of creation was spoken. This Hebrew word merachefet suggests a bird brooding over a nest, a creative presence bringing order and life out of formlessness and darkness.

The Holy Spirit is not a Pentecost invention. He is the breath of God that animates every living thing, the one through whom the heavens were made, through whom life is sustained.

When we think of the Spirit only in terms of spiritual gifts and church meetings, we have shrunk him to a fraction of his actual scope. He is the Spirit of creation at work in every atom, every breath, every moment of new life. The new humanity is not being animated by a religious experience. It is being indwelt by the one who made the world.

## Key Idea

The Holy Spirit is not confined to church meetings. He is the creative breath of God present at the beginning, sustaining all life, bringing order from chaos.

## Reflection

How does knowing the Spirit was present at creation change the way you think about his presence in your ordinary daily life, your work, your neighbourhood, your body?

## Today's Practice

Go outside today even for five minutes. Look at something alive: a tree, the sky, a person. Say quietly: 'The Spirit of God is at work here.' Practice noticing the Spirit beyond the church building.
