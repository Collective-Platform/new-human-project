## Today's Focus

The Spirit is the one who carries the New Human Project forward until the day of completion.

## Reading Notes

We have come a long way. In Ephesians we saw the blueprint of the new humanity: chosen, sealed, reconciled, built together as a holy temple. In Philippians we saw what it looks like to live that vision under pressure with joy, humility, peace, and generosity. And now we have met the one who makes it all possible: the Holy Spirit. He is the breath of God in the building. He is the person who takes the purpose of God and works them into the fabric of real human lives slowly, from the inside out, one degree of glory to another.

Paul's prayer in Ephesians 3 is the right place to end: that you may be strengthened with power through his Spirit in your inner being, that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith, that you may be filled with all the fullness of God.

This is the Spirit's agenda. Not experiences. Not performances. Fullness.

## Key Idea

The Spirit is not the power source for a religious programme. He is the person of God at work in the new humanity, forming Christ in us, together, until the day of completion.

## Reflection

After 25 days on the Holy Spirit and the studies of Ephesians and Philippians before this, what is the one thing you most want to carry forward? Name it. Commit to it.

## Today's Practice

Gather with at least two others from your community. Share one thing the Spirit has done in you through this study. Pray Ephesians 3:14-21 together out loud, slowly as your commissioning into the next chapter of the New Human Project.
