## Today's Focus

Patience and kindness are the fruits that only reveal themselves over time in ordinary, sustained relationships.

## Reading Notes

God's kindness is meant to lead you to repentance. This is stunning: the primary tool of God's transformation in human hearts is not power or judgment but patient kindness. And he asks us to put on (as the chosen, the holy, the beloved) compassionate hearts, kindness, humility, meekness, patience.

Patience in Greek is makrothumia long-tempered. Not the person who never gets frustrated, but the person whose fuse is genuinely long because they have been learning to hold people the way God holds them.

Kindness is chrestotes a generosity of spirit toward people who don't deserve it and can't repay it.

These fruits are humbling precisely because they only become visible over months and years of ordinary, unspectacular relationship.

## Key Idea

Patience and kindness are the Spirit's long-game fruits. They only become visible over time and they are more countercultural than any spiritual gift.

## Reflection

With whom in your life are you currently running out of patience? What would it look like to ask the Spirit for makrothumia (a genuinely longer fuse) specifically for them?

## Today's Practice

Identify the relationship in which you find it hardest to be patient. Every time impatience rises this week, say internally: 'Spirit, give me God's patience for this person.' Track what changes.
