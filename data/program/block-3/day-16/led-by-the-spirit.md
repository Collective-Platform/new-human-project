## Today's Focus

What does it mean to be led by the Spirit and how is it different from demanding direction?

## Reading Notes

Those who are led by the Spirit of God are sons of God. The language of being led is gentle and relational, it suggests the image of a shepherd guiding, not a system dispensing instructions.

The Spirit leads through the renewed mind, through Scripture, through wise community, through the deepening knowledge of God's character, through conscience, through the gifts of discernment.

Guidance is not primarily a dramatic moment of supernatural direction. It is the slow formation of a mind that thinks in Christ's categories. As you grow in the knowledge of God through his Word, through prayer, through community, your instincts are shaped, your judgments are refined, and the leading of the Spirit becomes less a dramatic event and more a natural orientation.

You are led as you walk, not before you begin.

## Key Idea

Being led by the Spirit is not about receiving supernatural directions. It is about becoming the kind of person whose instincts (formed by the Spirit) naturally move toward God.

## Reflection

How do you currently make decisions? How much does Scripture, wise community, and prayer shape your instincts as compared to waiting for a dramatic leading?

## Today's Practice

Bring one decision you are currently facing. Work through it using: What does Scripture say about this? What do wise people in my community think? What does this look like through the lens of who I am becoming in Christ? Ask the Spirit to guide through all three.
