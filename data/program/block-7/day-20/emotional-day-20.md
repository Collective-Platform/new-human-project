**Mark 6:30–32**

## Reading Notes

After a demanding time of ministry, Jesus invited His disciples to come away and rest.

We also have limits. We need sleep, nourishment, quiet, and support. When these needs are repeatedly neglected, we may become more irritable, tearful, or overwhelmed.

Sometimes, the next helpful step is very practical: eat a proper meal, take a break, ask for help, or have an honest conversation about your workload.

Serving faithfully includes caring for the life God has entrusted to you.

Your need for rest deserves attention before you reach exhaustion.

## Practice

Choose one action that supports rest today. You might finish work on time, take a quiet walk, or ask someone to share a responsibility.

## Reflection

What expectation or commitment needs adjusting?
