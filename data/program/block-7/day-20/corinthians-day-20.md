## Today's Focus

See diversity and mutual belonging as essential to the body of Christ.

## Reading Notes

Paul develops the body image: many members form one body, and by one Spirit believers were baptised into it across ethnic and social distinctions. No member can exclude itself for feeling inferior, and no member can dismiss another as unnecessary. God has arranged the body so that weaker and less honoured parts receive special care, preventing division and creating mutual concern. Suffering and honour are shared. The New Human Project is not uniformity; it is a Spirit-formed body in which difference serves belonging.

## Key Idea

Every member belongs, every member is needed, and honour should flow especially toward those easily overlooked.

## Reflection

Do you more often feel unnecessary or act as though someone else is unnecessary?

## Today's Practice

Contact someone whose contribution is easily missed and tell them specifically why they matter.
