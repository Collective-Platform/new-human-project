## Today's Focus

Read Paul's pastoral guidance on intimacy, marriage, and separation carefully.

## Reading Notes

Responding to questions from Corinth, Paul affirms mutual responsibility within marriage and allows temporary abstinence only by mutual agreement for prayer. He also values singleness as a gift, while recognising that people have different gifts. His teaching on divorce reflects Jesus' command, and he addresses marriages in which only one spouse believes, urging the believer not to leave if the other spouse is willing to remain. The passage resists both sexual self-indulgence and the idea that abstinence is spiritually superior for everyone.

## Key Idea

Faithfulness takes different forms, but marriage and singleness both belong under the lordship of Christ.

## Reflection

Based on the passage above, what do you think you can do better in your current status, single or married to glorify God?

## Today's Practice

Pray for faithfulness, peace, and mutual care in the households and single lives represented in your church.
