## Today's Focus

Distinguish the gospel's power from impressive performance.

## Reading Notes

Paul recalls proclaiming Jesus Christ crucified without relying on polished rhetoric to create faith. His aim was not anti-intellectualism; he does speak wisdom among the mature. But God's wisdom is the once-hidden plan centred in the crucified Lord, now revealed through the Spirit. The Spirit searches the depths of God and enables believers to receive what God has freely given. Spiritual understanding is therefore a gift, not a ground for pride. The chapter calls the church to humble dependence on God's self-revelation.

## Key Idea

God's wisdom is known because the Spirit reveals what God has accomplished in the crucified Christ.

## Reflection

Where are you tempted to confuse spiritual maturity with eloquence, confidence, or intelligence?

## Today's Practice

Before reading Scripture today, ask the Spirit for humility, attention, and understanding.
