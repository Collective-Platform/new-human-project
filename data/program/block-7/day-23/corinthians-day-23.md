## Today's Focus

Stand in the truth about Jesus' death and resurrection.

## Reading Notes

Paul reminds the Corinthians of the gospel they received, in which they stand, and by which they are being saved if they hold firmly to it. He recites an early summary: Christ died for sins, was buried, was raised on the third day, and appeared to many witnesses. Paul's own encounter came last, and he remembers both his former persecution and God's grace at work in his labour. The message is not a private spiritual idea but a proclaimed claim about God's action in Jesus, received and handed on by a community of witnesses.

## Key Idea

The church stands in the received gospel that Christ died, was buried, was raised, and appeared to witnesses.

## Reflection

Which part of Paul's gospel summary do you most need to hold firmly today?

## Today's Practice

Write verses 3-5 in your own words without removing any of their central claims.
