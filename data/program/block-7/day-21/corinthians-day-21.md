## Today's Focus

Let love define the value and use of every gift.

## Reading Notes

Paul places love at the centre of his discussion of spiritual gifts. Speech, knowledge, faith, generosity, and sacrifice can amount to nothing when love is absent. He describes love through patient, truthful, enduring action and by rejecting envy, boasting, arrogance, irritability, and scorekeeping. Gifts and present knowledge are partial and temporary; love belongs to the maturity toward which God is bringing his people. Faith, hope, and love remain, and love is greatest because it reflects God's enduring purpose for relationships.

## Key Idea

No spiritual ability, sacrifice, or achievement can substitute for patient, truthful, self-giving love.

## Reflection

Which description of love in verses 4-7 most directly exposes an area where you need to grow?

## Today's Practice

Turn that description into one specific action toward a real person today.
