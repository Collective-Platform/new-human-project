## Today's Focus

Let the church's future identity reshape present conflict.

## Reading Notes

Believers are taking one another before public courts over ordinary disputes. Paul considers this a failure of communal wisdom and witness. Their readiness to wrong and defraud one another contradicts the kingdom they are to inherit. His list of former patterns is followed by a decisive reminder: they were washed, sanctified, and justified in the name of Jesus and by God's Spirit. The point is neither superiority nor denial of past sin; it is that grace creates a changed identity and a new way of handling relationships.

## Key Idea

People washed and made holy in Christ should pursue justice without exploiting one another.

## Reflection

When conflict arises, is your first goal to win, to protect yourself, or to seek what is right?

## Today's Practice

Take one step toward resolving a manageable conflict directly, honestly, and peacefully.
