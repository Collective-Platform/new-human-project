## Today's Focus

Learn from Paul's willingness to surrender legitimate rights for the gospel.

## Reading Notes

Paul defends his apostleship and his genuine right to material support, drawing on ordinary work, the law of Moses, temple practice, and Jesus' instruction. Yet he has chosen not to use that right in Corinth so that no obstacle is placed before the gospel. His adaptability to different groups does not mean ingenuity or moral compromise; it is purposeful identification in order to save some souls. The athletic images of running and boxing emphasise a disciplined and intentional life rather than a life of entitlement.

## Key Idea

Gospel freedom includes the freedom to surrender a right for the sake of others.

## Reflection

Which legitimate right or preference is hardest for you to release when love requires it?

## Today's Practice

Choose one convenience you can set aside this week to make the gospel or another person's good more accessible.
