## Today's Focus

Test choices by benefit, conscience, witness, and love.

## Reading Notes

Paul again qualifies the slogan that everything is permitted: not everything is beneficial or constructive. Believers should seek another person's good, receive ordinary food with gratitude, and avoid needless anxiety. When food is explicitly identified as sacrificial, however, the other person's conscience becomes relevant. Paul's summary is broad: do everything for God's glory, give no unnecessary offence, and seek the salvation of many rather than personal advantage. His own pattern is worth imitating only as he imitates Christ.

## Key Idea

Mature freedom asks not only whether an action is allowed, but whether it glorifies God and serves people.

## Reflection

What current decision needs more than the question, Can I do this?

## Today's Practice

Before one choice today, ask: Is it beneficial, constructive, loving, and honouring to God?
