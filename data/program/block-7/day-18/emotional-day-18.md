**Romans 8:1; 1 John 1:9**

## Reading Notes

There is a difference between saying, “I did something wrong,” and deciding, “Everything about me is wrong.”

When we have sinned, we can confess, seek forgiveness, and make amends. Shame can keep us hiding, convinced that we are beyond help.

In Christ, we are invited to come into the light. God’s grace gives us courage to face what happened and begin responding differently.

**Your mistakes matter, and so does God’s mercy.** You can take responsibility without continually punishing yourself.

**A failure is something to address; it does not have to become your whole identity.**

## Practice

Bring one regret to God. Identify a faithful next step: confessing, apologising, repairing harm, or accepting forgiveness you have already received.

## Reflection

What would receiving grace help me do today?
