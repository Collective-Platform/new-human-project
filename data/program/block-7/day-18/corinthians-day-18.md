## Today's Focus

Recognise Christ's body at the table by caring for his people.

## Reading Notes

The Corinthians' meal exposes social divisions: some eat abundantly while others go hungry. Paul says such gatherings do more harm than good. He passes on the tradition of Jesus taking bread and the cup, commanding remembrance and proclaiming the Lord's death until he comes. To eat with an ungodly attitude is not a demand for personal perfection but a warning against participating in a manner that contradicts the meal's meaning. They must examine themselves, discern the body, wait for one another, and refuse to humiliate those who have little.

## Key Idea

The Lord's Supper proclaims the cross truthfully when the gathered church receives one another as one body.

## Reflection

What are some thoughts or actions you had when having a shared meal that would be deemed unworthy?

## Today's Practice

At your next shared meal or Communion gathering, intentionally notice and welcome someone who could be overlooked.
