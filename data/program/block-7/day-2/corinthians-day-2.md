## Today's Focus

See why loyalty to Christian personalities damages the church's unity.

## Reading Notes

Reports from Chloe's household reveal quarrels in Corinth. Groups identify themselves with Paul, Apollos, Cephas, or Christ, turning respected leaders into badges of status. Paul answers with direct questions: Christ is not divided, Paul was not crucified for them, and they were not baptised into Paul's name. Baptism belongs to allegiance to Christ, not to a human faction. The apostle does not erase differences among leaders; he refuses to let those differences replace the shared centre of the gospel.

## Key Idea

Christian leaders may serve the church, but only Christ can be its centre.

## Reflection

What labels, preferences, or personalities are most likely to divide Christians in your setting?

## Today's Practice

Speak appreciatively today about a Christian community or leader outside your usual circle.
