## Today's Focus

Let the cross redefine wisdom, power, and status.

## Reading Notes

The message of a crucified Messiah appeared foolish to those seeking impressive wisdom and weak to those demanding powerful signs. Paul insists that God's saving action overturns those measures. The Corinthians' own calling illustrates the point: few were powerful or socially distinguished, yet God chose what the world dismissed so that no one could boast before him. Christ has become wisdom, righteousness, holiness, and redemption for them. In the new humanity, worth is received from Christ rather than secured through achievement or rank.

## Key Idea

The cross overturns human boasting and makes Christ the source of the church's life.

## Reflection

Which form of success or respectability most tempts you to measure people by the wrong standard?

## Today's Practice

Thank God for one apparent weakness through which you have learned dependence on Christ.
