**Psalm 62:8**

Trust in him at all times, you people;    pour out your hearts to him,    for God is our refuge.

## Reading Notes

Have you ever edited a prayer because it sounded too angry, too sad, or too messy?

We may think that approaching God means knowing the right words. Yet the Psalms contain questions, tears, frustration, and longing. God invites us to pour out our hearts before Him.

You can tell Him, “I am disappointed.” You can admit, “I do not understand.” You can pray, “I am struggling to trust You.”

Honesty gives us a place to begin. From there, we can listen, receive comfort, and allow God to shape our response.

You do not have to tidy up your emotions before bringing them to God.

## Practice

Write an honest prayer beginning with: “God, what I really want to say is…” Avoid correcting your words as you write.

## Reflection

What makes me hesitate to be honest with Him?
