**Psalm 139:23–24**

## Reading Notes

“How are you?”

“Fine.”

Sometimes, that answer comes out before we have even checked how we are really doing. We may be so busy caring for others and completing tasks that we overlook what is happening within us.

David invited God to search his heart. We can make that same invitation today. God already knows us, and He welcomes our honest attention.

You may feel several things at once: grateful and tired, hopeful and worried, loved and lonely. You do not have to sort everything out immediately. Begin by noticing.

**Naming a feeling helps us understand what needs our attention.**

## Practice

Pause for two minutes without your phone. Take a few slow breaths. Complete this sentence: “God, today I feel \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ because \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_.”

## Reflection

What have I been too busy to notice about myself?
