## Today's Focus

Begin with what God has already given his church in Christ.

## Reading Notes

Paul writes to God's church in Corinth, a diverse congregation in a prominent Roman city. Before correcting its serious problems, he names the believers as sanctified, called to be holy, enriched in speech and knowledge, and sustained by God. Their identity rests in God's grace and in fellowship with Jesus Christ. This opening does not deny their failures; it places correction inside God's faithful call. The New Human Project begins with people whom God has gathered around Christ, not people who have already perfected themselves.

## Key Idea

The church faces its failures from within the grace and calling God has already given in Christ.

## Reflection

When you think about your church, do its weaknesses or God's faithful calling shape your first response?

## Today's Practice

Read verses 4-9 slowly and list every gift, action, or promise attributed to God.
