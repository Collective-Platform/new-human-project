## MOVEMENT 1 — New Foundation

# Christ crucified as the foundation of a united church

Days 1-6 · 1 Corinthians 1:1-4:7

---

Paul begins with a divided church and directs it back to Jesus Christ. These six days explore calling, the wisdom of the cross, the work of the Spirit, and ministry understood as faithful stewardship rather than status.
