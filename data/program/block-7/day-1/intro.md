## How to Use This Study Plan

What does the gospel look like inside a gifted but divided church? First Corinthians answers with unusual honesty. Paul addresses factions, status, sexuality, marriage, freedom, worship, spiritual gifts, love, resurrection, money, and mission - repeatedly bringing the church back to Jesus Christ and the good of his people.

Over 25 days, we will follow the letter's argument and explore how the cross and resurrection form a new humanity: one body made holy in Christ, diverse in its gifts, responsible in its freedom, and steadfast in love.

Each day includes:

- Today’s Focus - A single clear aim to keep you anchored as you read.

- Reading Notes - Context and explanation drawn closely from the passage.

- Key Idea - One sentence that captures the heart of the day.

- Reflection - A question for honest personal or group conversation.

- Today’s Practice - A concrete response to the text.

## Four Movements

The letter can be studied through four broad movements. The sections overlap, but together they trace Paul's response from the church's foundation in Christ to its shared life, worship, hope, and mission.

- **MOVEMENT 1** — New Foundation · Days 1-6
  Christ crucified as the foundation of a united church.
- **MOVEMENT 2** — New Holiness · Days 7-13
  Belonging to Christ in leadership, body, relationships, and freedom.
- **MOVEMENT 3** — New Freedom · Days 14-20
  Rights and gifts directed toward the good of the community.
- **MOVEMENT 4** — New Body · Days 21-25
  Love, ordered worship, resurrection, and shared mission.
