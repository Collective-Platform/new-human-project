## Today's Focus

Receive the body as part of God's redemptive claim on your life.

## Reading Notes

Paul quotes Corinthian slogans about freedom and answers each one. Not everything permitted is beneficial, and freedom must not become enslavement. The body is not disposable; it is for the Lord, will be raised by God, and is a member of Christ. Sexual union carries bodily significance, so sexual immorality contradicts belonging to Christ. Because the body is a temple of the Holy Spirit and has been bought at a price, believers are to glorify God in their bodies. Christian discipleship is embodied, not merely inward.

## Key Idea

The body matters to God because it belongs to Christ, houses the Spirit, and is destined for resurrection.

## Reflection

What habit most clearly reveals whether you treat your body as belonging to God?

## Today's Practice

Choose one concrete way to honour God physically today through rest, restraint, care, or integrity.
