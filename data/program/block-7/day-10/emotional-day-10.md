**Galatians 5:22–25**

## Reading Notes

We cannot always choose our first feeling. We can learn to choose what we do next.

When we feel insecure, we can ask for clarity. When we feel frustrated, we can speak with patience. When we feel lonely, we can reach out to someone safe.

The fruit of the Spirit grows as we walk with Him in ordinary situations. Growth may look like one calmer conversation, one honest request, or one apology offered sooner.

You may still feel upset while choosing a loving response.

**Emotional growth often happens through small, repeated choices.**

## Practice

Choose one quality from Galatians 5:22–23 to practise today. Make it specific: “In this conversation, I will practise patience by listening before I answer.”

## Reflection

Where can I practise it today?
