**Philippians 1:6; Psalm 42:5**

## Reading Notes

Over these 25 days, you have made space to listen to your heart and bring your emotions to God.

You may notice a pattern more clearly. Perhaps you have recognised how tiredness affects you, named a hidden disappointment, or found the courage to ask for support.

You may also still be struggling. That does not erase the value of what you have practised.

Emotional growth takes time. Continue noticing, praying, seeking truth, receiving care, and choosing helpful responses. God remains present throughout the process.

**You can have a difficult day and still be growing.**

## Practice

Review your daily entries. Notice recurring emotions, situations that affected you, and practices that helped. Choose one habit to continue for the next two weeks.

## Reflection

What would I like to keep bringing to God?
