## Today's Focus

Bring resurrection hope into money, plans, relationships, and service.

## Reading Notes

The final chapter is practical because we see how resurrection hope shapes ordinary life. Paul encourages regular giving to help others, flexible planning under God's guidance, and genuine respect for fellow servants. Even when ministry faces obstacles, believers are called to stay alert, stand firm, act with courage, and do everything in love.

## Key Idea

Resurrection-shaped faith becomes visible in planned generosity, shared responsibility, courage, and love.

## Reflection

After 25 days, what change from 1 Corinthians most needs to become part of your church's shared life?

## Today's Practice

Choose one concrete commitment in generosity, unity, holiness, worship, or service and share it with someone who can encourage you.
