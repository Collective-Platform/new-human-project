## Today's Focus

Measure leadership by the pattern of Christ rather than public prestige.

## Reading Notes

With sharp irony, Paul contrasts the Corinthians' sense of triumph with the apostles' costly service. The apostles endure hunger, mistreatment, hard work, and dishonour; when abused, they bless, and when persecuted, they endure. Paul writes as a spiritual father seeking to correct, not humiliate. His example shows that Christian authority is not theatre or self-promotion but a life consistent with the crucified Christ. The kingdom of God is demonstrated in effective, faithful service rather than lofty, empty claims.

## Key Idea

Gospel leadership carries authority through faithful, Christlike service rather than status.

## Reflection

Personally, what qualities do you associate with strong leadership, and is it similar to what you find in this passage?

## Today's Practice

Encourage a leader whose quiet faithfulness may be overlooked.
