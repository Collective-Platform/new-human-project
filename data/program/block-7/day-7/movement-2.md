## MOVEMENT 2 — New Holiness

# Belonging to Christ in leadership, body, relationships, and freedom

Days 7-13 · 1 Corinthians 4:8-8:13

---

The gospel reaches into leadership, embodied life, difficult relationships, and Christian freedom. These seven days follow Paul's instructions on costly service, accountability, disputes, sexual integrity, marriage, singleness, faithful calling, and love for another person's conscience.
