## Today's Focus

Receive Israel's story as a warning to flee idolatry.

## Reading Notes

Israel experienced divine guidance, provision, and covenant privileges, yet many fell through idolatry, sexual immorality, testing God, and grumbling. Paul says these events were written as warnings for the church. Spiritual experience does not make anyone immune to unfaithfulness, so we must be watchful even as believers. God remains faithful and provides a way to endure temptation. Participation in the Lord's cup and table signifies communion with Christ and his people, making participation in idolatrous worship incompatible with allegiance to him.

## Key Idea

Shared spiritual privileges call for humble vigilance, exclusive loyalty to God, and trust in his faithfulness.

## Reflection

What good thing is most likely to become ultimate in your life and compete for the loyalty owed to God?

## Today's Practice

Identify one recurring temptation and write down the practical way of endurance God has made available.
