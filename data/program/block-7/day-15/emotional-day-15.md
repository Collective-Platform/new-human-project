**Romans 12:2**

2 Do not conform to the pattern of this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind. Then you will be able to test and approve what God’s will is—his good, pleasing and perfect will.

## Reading Notes

Someone replies late, and we think, “They do not care.”

We make a mistake, and we conclude, “I always fail.”

Our feelings are real, but our first explanation may be incomplete. Past experiences, tiredness, and fear can influence how we interpret a situation.

Renewing our minds includes learning to examine these interpretations. What actually happened? What am I assuming? Is there another reasonable explanation?

This does not mean dismissing hurtful behaviour. It helps us respond to what we know, with room for clarity.

**A strong feeling does not require an immediate conclusion.**

## Practice

Write down one upsetting thought. Under it, write:

* What I know happened.  
* What I am assuming.  
* A more balanced way to understand the situation.
