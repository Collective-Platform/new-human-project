## Today's Focus

Seek undivided devotion to the Lord in your actual circumstances.

## Reading Notes

Paul repeats a principle: believers should ordinarily live faithfully in the situation in which God called them. Circumcision does not determine standing before God, and enslaved believers possess freedom in the Lord even as Paul tells them to take freedom if the opportunity arises. Because the present world is passing, marriage, grief, joy, possessions, and commerce must not become ultimate. Paul's preference for singleness in the present circumstances is pastoral advice aimed at undivided devotion, not a universal command or denial of marriage's goodness.

## Key Idea

Calling is not an excuse for passivity; it is an invitation to faithful devotion within real circumstances.

## Reflection

Are you postponing obedience until your circumstances change?

## Today's Practice

Name one act of faithfulness available to you today, without waiting for a different season.
