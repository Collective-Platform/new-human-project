**Philippians 4:6–9**

## Reading Notes

Worry often begins with “What if?”

What if I fail? What if things get worse? What if I cannot manage?

Our minds can travel far ahead while our bodies remain in today. Paul encourages us to bring our requests to God with thanksgiving. We can tell God what concerns us and ask for help with the next step.

Prayer does not mean every concern disappears immediately. We may need to return to God many times with the same worry.

When worry returns, you can pray again without judging yourself.

## Practice

Write down your worries. Beside each one, identify either a helpful action you can take or something you need to entrust to God. Choose one manageable action for today.

## Reflection

What is within my responsibility today?
