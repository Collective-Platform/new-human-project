## Today's Focus

Take serious wrongdoing seriously while seeking the community's integrity.

## Reading Notes

Paul addresses a public sexual relationship that even the surrounding culture regarded as unacceptable. His concern includes both the offender's conduct and the congregation's prideful tolerance. The command to remove the person is a severe act of communal discipline whose stated aim includes eventual salvation, not revenge. Paul reiterates that we are called to be God’s witness outside the church. He distinguishes accountability inside the church from judging society at large.

## Key Idea

Grace does not make destructive conduct unimportant; it forms a community committed to truth, restoration, and holiness.

## Reflection

Why can both passive tolerance and harsh condemnation fail to reflect the purpose of Christian discipline?

## Today's Practice

Ask God to show you where repentance, wise accountability, or a restorative conversation is needed.
