## Today's Focus

Approach a difficult passage with attention to worship, honour, and cultural setting.

## Reading Notes

Paul uses first-century dress customs to teach a bigger lesson about honour in worship. While specific cultural details like head coverings may feel distant today, the main message is clear: women actively pray and speak in church, men and women rely on each other, and everything ultimately comes from God. Instead of getting bogged down in ancient cultural rules, our main focus should be worshipping God with respect and valuing the community around us.

## Key Idea

Gathered worship should honour God and one another while acknowledging our mutual dependence.

## Reflection

How can humility guide you when faithful Christians interpret a difficult passage differently?

## Today's Practice

Read the passage twice and mark what is clear, what is culturally located, and what requires further study.
