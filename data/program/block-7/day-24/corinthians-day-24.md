## Today's Focus

Understand why bodily resurrection is essential to Christian hope.

## Reading Notes

Some Corinthians deny the resurrection of the dead. Paul argues that this denial unravels the gospel: if the dead are not raised, Christ has not been raised, preaching and faith are empty, and those who died in Christ have perished. But Christ has been raised as the firstfruits, guaranteeing a wider harvest. Paul describes continuity and transformation through the image of a seed and speaks of a body raised imperishable and glorious. God's final victory over death gives present work lasting meaning.

## Key Idea

Because Christ has been raised, death will be defeated and faithful labour in the Lord is not in vain.

## Reflection

How would a merely spiritual hope differ from Paul's promise of embodied resurrection and renewed life?

## Today's Practice

Encourage someone facing grief or weariness with the hope expressed in verses 54-58.
