## Today's Focus

Use freedom in ways that build others up.

## Reading Notes

Food associated with idols raised practical questions in Corinth. Paul agrees that idols have no real divine existence and that there is one God and one Lord, Jesus Christ. Yet correct knowledge alone can make people proud, while love builds up. Some believers still connect such food with former worship, so another person's freedom can wound their conscience, bringing shame and guilt. Paul does not call ignorance virtuous; he requires knowledge to be governed by love and by concern for the brother or sister for whom Christ died.

## Key Idea

Christian knowledge reaches maturity when it becomes loving responsibility for another person's good.

## Reflection

Where might your confidence in being right prevent you from noticing the effect of your choices on someone else?

## Today's Practice

Voluntarily limit one nonessential freedom today if doing so will protect or encourage another person.
