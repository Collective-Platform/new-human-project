**James 1:19–20; Ephesians 4:26–27**

## Reading Notes

Anger can rise quickly when we feel dismissed, treated unfairly, or pushed beyond our limits.

It deserves attention. However, feeling angry does not permit us to wound someone with our words or actions.

Scripture teaches us to listen carefully and be slow to speak. A pause creates room to understand what happened and choose a wise response.

Ask yourself: “What is underneath this anger? Am I hurt, afraid, exhausted, or concerned about something wrong?”

You can take anger seriously while taking responsibility for your response.

## Practice

When anger rises, pause before replying. If needed, say: “I want to talk about this, but I need a little time to settle first.” Return to the conversation when you can speak respectfully.
