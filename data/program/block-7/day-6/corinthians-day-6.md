## Today's Focus

Replace boasting with faithful stewardship.

## Reading Notes

Paul warns against self-deception through worldly wisdom and against boasting in particular leaders. Because believers belong to Christ, the ministries of Paul, Apollos, and Cephas are gifts for the whole church rather than competing possessions. Apostles should be regarded as servants of Christ and stewards entrusted with God's mysteries. A steward's central requirement is faithfulness, and final judgment belongs to the Lord, who knows hidden motives. Every gift has been received, so boasting as though it were self-produced makes no sense.

## Key Idea

Christian service is stewardship: received from God, exercised for others, and finally assessed by the Lord.

## Reflection

Where do comparison and the desire for recognition affect the way you serve?

## Today's Practice

Do one useful act of service today without drawing attention to yourself.
