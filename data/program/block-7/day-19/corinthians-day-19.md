## Today's Focus

Receive spiritual gifts as diverse works of the one Triune God.

## Reading Notes

Paul wants the Corinthians to be informed about spiritual gifts. The basic confession that Jesus is Lord is enabled by the Holy Spirit. Gifts, forms of service, and activities differ, but the same Spirit, Lord, and God are at work. Each manifestation of the Spirit is given for the common good, not as a badge of rank or private possession. Paul lists varied gifts and closes by stressing the Spirit's freedom in distributing them. The passage directs attention away from competition and toward God's purpose for the whole community.

## Key Idea

The Spirit gives diverse abilities to different people so the whole church may benefit.

## Reflection

Do you tend to compare gifts, rank them, or receive them gratefully for the common good?

## Today's Practice

Affirm one gift you see in another person and name how it strengthens the community.
