## Today's Focus

Hold intelligibility, participation, and order together in gathered worship.

## Reading Notes

It can be easy to measure spiritual passion by intensity, noise, or personal emotion. But Paul reminds us that the true test of spiritual maturity isn't how dramatically we worship and experience God. Rather it's how effectively we love and serve those around us. When we gather with other believers, our focus shifts from "What am I getting out of this?" to "How can I build up someone else today?" This mindset changes how we speak, listen, and serve.

God values intelligibility or clarity because He values community. Words that are confused or misunderstood cannot heal, instruct, or comfort. When our speech is thoughtful, clear, and gracious, it creates a welcoming space where people can meet God and grow together. Order and self-control aren't barriers to the Holy Spirit; they are the natural fruits of a Spirit-led life that seeks peace over self-display.

## Key Idea

Gathered worship should welcome meaningful participation while guiding every contribution toward peace and edification.

## Reflection

Which is harder for you to value in worship: passionate participation, clear understanding, or wise order?

## Today's Practice

Prepare one clear, truthful word of encouragement that can build someone else up.
