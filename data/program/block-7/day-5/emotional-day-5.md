**John 11:32–36**

## Reading Notes

Jesus stood with people who were grieving, and He wept.

Although He knew He would raise Lazarus, He still entered their sorrow. His tears show us something about His compassion.

Sadness may come from a death, a broken relationship, an unmet hope, or a change we did not choose. Sometimes we grieve something others cannot see.

You do not need to rush yourself towards a cheerful answer. Bring your sadness to Jesus. Allow yourself to acknowledge what mattered and what hurts.

**Tears can be part of a faithful response to loss.**

## Practice

Name one loss or disappointment you have been carrying. Spend a few quiet minutes telling Jesus what you miss. If helpful, share it with someone you trust.

## Reflection

What sadness needs space in my life?
