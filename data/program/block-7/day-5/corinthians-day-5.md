## Today's Focus

Understand ministry as shared service under God.

## Reading Notes

Jealousy and rivalry show that the Corinthians are still behaving according to ordinary status competition. Paul and Apollos are servants assigned different tasks: one plants, another waters, but God gives the growth. Paul then changes images. The church is God's building, founded on Jesus Christ, and each builder must be careful how he builds the church because one day, each man’s work will be tested. Finally, the gathered community is God's temple because the Spirit dwells among them. Damaging that community is therefore a serious matter. No worker owns the church; all are accountable to God for how they build.

## Key Idea

The church belongs to God, grows by God, and must be built carefully on Jesus Christ.

## Reflection

Do you usually notice the human worker or the God who gives growth?

## Today's Practice

Pray for another person who serves your church in a different ministry/role. Ask God to reveal how both of you can work together better in building His church.
