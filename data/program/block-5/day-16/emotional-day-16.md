**Matthew 5:9**

Blessed are the peacemakers, for they will be called children of God.

## Reading Notes

Romans 12:18 \- **18** If it is possible, as far as it depends on you, live at peace with everyone.

Become a person of PEACE.

Jesus said: “Blessed are the peacemakers.” \- Matthew 5:9

Our emotional state doesn't stay only within us. We carry it into our conversations, families, workplaces, friendships, and communities.

Stress can spread. Anger can spread. But **peace can spread too**.

Being a peacemaker doesn't mean avoiding difficult conversations or keeping everyone happy. Romans 12:18 reminds us to live at peace with everyone **as far as it depends on us**.

Sometimes peace looks like listening before responding. Sometimes it means apologising. Sometimes it means speaking truth calmly.

Sometimes it means refusing to participate in unnecessary conflict.

The Holy Spirit forms peace within us so that peace can flow through us.

You cannot control another person's response, but you can choose what you bring into the interaction.

Emotional flourishing means becoming increasingly aware:

**“What am I carrying—and what am I giving to others?”**

## Practice

Before your next meeting, conversation, or interaction, pause for ten seconds.

## Reflection

What emotional atmosphere do you usually bring into a room?
