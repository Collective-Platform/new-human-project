## MOVEMENT 2 — How He Lived

# The way Jesus moved through the world

Days 8–13 · Luke 4–7 · Mark 4–6 · John 4

---

The identity of Jesus is not an abstraction. It became visible in the specific, daily way he moved through the world. How he treated people. Who he stopped for. Where he went. How he prayed. The Gospels are not primarily theological treatises; they are accounts of a life, a life that was so consistent, so surprising, so full of grace and truth, that the people who watched it could not look away. These six days attend to the texture of Jesus's daily life: to the way he lived rather than only what he said.
