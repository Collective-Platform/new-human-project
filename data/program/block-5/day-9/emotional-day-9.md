**Romans 8:5–6; John 15:9–11**

## Reading Notes

Where your mind goes matters. “The mind governed by the Spirit is life and peace.” \- Romans 8:6

What we repeatedly give our attention to eventually shapes our inner world.

If our minds constantly feed on comparison, bad news, criticism, fear, or what we lack, it shouldn't surprise us when we feel restless and discouraged.

Paul invites us to **set our minds on what the Spirit desires**.

This doesn't mean ignoring problems or pretending everything is fine. It means becoming intentional about what we allow to occupy our thoughts.

Jesus gives us an even deeper foundation: “Remain in my love... I have told you this so that my joy may be in you.” \- John 15:9,11

Our emotional health is not sustained simply by thinking happier thoughts. It grows as we learn to **remain in Christ**.

The Holy Spirit continually redirects our attention toward Jesus—His love, His truth, His presence and His promises.

Sometimes we cannot change our circumstances today.

But we can ask: **“What am I feeding my mind in the middle of them?”**

What receives your attention receives room to grow. Feed fear, and fear grows. Feed comparison, and dissatisfaction grows.

Remain in Christ, and slowly His **life, peace and joy** begin shaping you from within.

## Practice

Write down the three things receiving most of your mental attention lately.

## Reflection

What helps you remain aware of Jesus during your day?
