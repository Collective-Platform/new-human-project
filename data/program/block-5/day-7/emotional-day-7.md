**Romans 12:2; Matthew 6:22–23**

## Reading Notes

Your thoughts shape your inner world.   
“Be transformed by the renewing of your mind.”  \- Romans 12:2

Not every thought that enters your mind deserves to stay there.

Sometimes we repeatedly tell ourselves:

**“I'm not good enough.”**  
**“Something will go wrong.”**  
**“They probably don't like me.”**  
**“I'll never change.”**

When repeated long enough, thoughts can begin to feel like truth—and what we believe affects how we feel, respond, and live.

Jesus taught that the “eye is the lamp of the body” (Matthew 6:22). What we continually focus on influences how we see everything else.

Renewal, therefore, isn't simply about thinking positively. It is learning to see ourselves, others, and our circumstances through **God's truth**.

The Holy Spirit helps us recognise thoughts that are shaped by fear, insecurity, comparison, or past experiences and redirects us toward what is true.

You may not control every thought that enters your mind, but you can decide **which thoughts you continue feeding**.

Transformation happens as we repeatedly allow God's truth to reshape our thinking.

## Practice

Write one truth you want to carry today.
