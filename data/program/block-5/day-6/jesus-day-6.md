## Today's Focus

What kind of authority does Jesus exercise and how is it different from every other kind of authority the world offers?

## Reading Notes

When Jesus finished the Sermon on the Mount, the crowds were astonished at his teaching, for he was teaching them as one who had authority, and not as their scribes. The scribes taught by appeal to tradition, quoting earlier teachers to support their words. Jesus taught differently: You have heard that it was said, but I say to you. He did not derive his authority from a chain of tradition; he spoke from within himself, as the source. His authority was not only verbal. In the synagogue at Capernaum, he commanded an unclean spirit with a word, and it obeyed him. The crowd responded: What is this? A new teaching, and with authority\! He commands even the unclean spirits. The authority Jesus exercises covers teaching, nature, disease, death, and spiritual forces. Every domain of created reality responds to his word. After the resurrection, he gathered his disciples and made the scope of that authority explicit: All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. Not some authority, or authority in religious matters only, but all authority in every realm, over every power, in this age and the age to come.

## Key Idea

_The authority of Jesus is not derived from any human source. It is his own, covering every domain of creation. And it is this authority that stands behind every promise he makes._

## Reflection

Which specific area of your life, whether a relationship, a habit, a fear, or your future, have you not yet fully placed under Jesus's authority? What would practical submission to his leadership in that area look like starting today?

## Today's Practice

Read Matthew 28:18-20 slowly. The commission flows from the authority: because all authority is his, therefore go. Write down one area of your life that needs to be brought under that authority today. Pray it over with Matthew 28:18 as your foundation.
