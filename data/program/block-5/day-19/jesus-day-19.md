## Today's Focus

What does Jesus give his disciples in John 14–17 to carry them through what is coming — and what does his prayer in John 17 reveal about what he most wants for his people?

## Reading Notes

In John 14 through 17, Jesus speaks at length to his disciples on the night before his crucifixion, knowing what is coming and knowing they do not yet understand. He gives them what they will need. He gives them a destination: In my Father's house there are many rooms, I go to prepare a place for you. He gives them a companion: the Father will give you another Helper, the Spirit of truth, who will be with you forever. He gives them peace: Peace I leave with you, my peace I give to you. Not as the world gives do I give to you. Let not your hearts be troubled, neither let them be afraid. He gives them an honest assessment of what lies ahead: In the world you will have tribulation. But take heart, I have overcome the world. Then he prays, and the prayer of John 17 is one of the most intimate and revealing passages in Scripture. He prays not only for his disciples, but for all who will believe through their word, which includes everyone reading these words. What he asks for them is not protection from difficulty, success, or influence, but one thing: that they may be one, even as we are one, so that the world may know that you sent me.

## Key Idea

_Jesus's final prayer is for unity and not uniformity, but the deep oneness of a community shaped by the love between the Father and the Son. This is the witness he entrusts to his people._

## Reflection

What does it mean to you that Jesus prayed specifically for you in John 17:20 and that you are included in the 'all who will believe through their word'? How does his prayer for unity change how you view your faith community?

## Today's Practice

Read John 17:20-23 slowly, inserting the name of your church community in the prayer. Then pray it back to God on behalf of your community, for the unity that Jesus prays for, and for the witness to the world that such unity produces.
