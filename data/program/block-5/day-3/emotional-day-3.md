**Matthew 12:34–35; Romans 7:15–25**

## Reading Notes

Have you ever reacted strongly to something and later wondered: **“Why did that bother me so much?”**

Someone makes a harmless comment, but you immediately become defensive. Someone doesn't respond to your message, and your mind starts thinking: *"Did I do something wrong?"*

Someone gives you feedback, and suddenly you feel like you're not good enough. Someone disagrees with you, and you feel the need to prove yourself.

Sometimes the situation is small, but the emotional reaction is huge.

Why? Because our reactions are rarely only about what is happening **in front of us**.

Sometimes they are connected to what is happening **inside us**.

Jesus said: “For the mouth speaks what the heart is full of.” \- Matthew 12:34

In other words, what comes out of us can reveal what is already happening within us.

Our words. Our reactions. Our defensiveness. Our impatience. Our withdrawal. Our anger.

Sometimes these things are simply the **surface**.

The deeper question is: **What is underneath them?**

## Practice

Think of **one strong emotional reaction** you've had recently. Complete this sentence: “This affected me so strongly because \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

## Reflection

Instead of asking only, “Why did they do that to me?”, what might change if you also asked, “Holy Spirit, what is this revealing in me?”
