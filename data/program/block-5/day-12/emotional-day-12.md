**Matthew 18:21–35**

## Reading Notes

Forgiveness is about freedom.

Peter asked Jesus, “How many times should I forgive?” Jesus’ answer showed that forgiveness is not about keeping score.

Forgiveness can be difficult because some wounds are deep. Jesus is not asking us to pretend the hurt never happened or to call something acceptable when it was wrong.

**Forgiveness acknowledges the wound but refuses to let bitterness have the final word.**

Sometimes we replay what happened, imagine what we wish we had said, or wait for the other person to make things right. Slowly, the hurt continues occupying space within us.

Forgiveness is surrendering our right to revenge and placing justice into God’s hands.

It also does not automatically mean reconciliation. Trust may need to be rebuilt, and healthy boundaries may still be necessary.

The Holy Spirit helps us do what may feel impossible: release the person while allowing God to heal our heart.

Forgiveness may happen in a moment, but emotional healing can take time.

## Practice

Name one hurt you are carrying. Open your hands and pray: **“Jesus, I give this hurt and this person to You. Heal what remains wounded in me.”**
