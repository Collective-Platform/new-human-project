## MOVEMENT 4 — The Cross, Resurrection, and Commission

# The events that change everything — and the life they make possible

Days 20–25 · Mark 14–16 · John 19–20 · Acts 1

---

Everything in the Gospels moves toward these final days: the entry into Jerusalem, the last supper, Gethsemane, the trial, the cross, the burial, the empty tomb, and the resurrection appearances. These are not simply the conclusion of a remarkable life. They are the events on which all reality turns. The cross is where the weight of human sin meets the love of God, and neither blinks. The resurrection is the Father's answer to everything the cross accomplished. The commission is the moment Jesus sends the new humanity, formed in his image and sealed by his Spirit, into the world he died to redeem.
