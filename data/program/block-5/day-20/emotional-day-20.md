**Galatians 5:22–23; John 15:4–5**

## Reading Notes

Fruit takes time. Jesus said: “If you remain in me and I in you, you will bear much fruit.” \- John 15:5

Emotional formation is not about never feeling angry, anxious, disappointed, or overwhelmed.

It is about becoming increasingly **Christlike in how we respond to those emotions**.

The fruit of the Spirit—love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control—is formed within us over time.

Fruit does not appear overnight.

It grows as we remain connected to Jesus and allow the Holy Spirit to work in the ordinary pressures of life.

Difficult people can form patience. Disappointment can deepen trust.

Conflict can teach gentleness. Waiting can develop self-control.

Instead of only asking God to remove uncomfortable situations, perhaps we can also ask: **“Holy Spirit, what are You forming in me through this?”**

Your difficult season does not have to be wasted. God can use it to shape your character.

## Practice

Choose one fruit of the Spirit you need most in your current season. Pray: “Holy Spirit, grow \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ in me today.” Look for one opportunity to practise it.

## Reflection

How might your current challenges be forming you?
