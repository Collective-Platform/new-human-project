**John 15:1–8; Romans 15:13**

## Reading Notes

Flourishing starts with remaining.

Jesus said: “Remain in me, as I also remain in you.” \- John 15:4

A flourishing life is not a life without difficult emotions, disappointment, or uncertainty.

It is a life that stays rooted in Jesus through them all.

Over these days, you have practised awareness, surrender, renewal, and formation. But emotional health is not a 15-day achievement. It is an ongoing journey of allowing the Holy Spirit to shape you.

Jesus reminds us that fruit grows through remaining.

We remain when we return to Him in anxiety. We remain when we surrender control. We remain when we renew unhealthy thoughts with truth. We remain when we choose a Spirit-led response instead of reacting.

Romans 15:13 reminds us that the Holy Spirit fills us with hope.

You don't have to manufacture emotional health by yourself.

Stay connected to Jesus. Keep paying attention.

Keep surrendering. Keep allowing the Spirit to form you.

And over time, fruit will grow.
