## Reading Notes

Create space between feeling and action.

When we feel hurt, criticised, ignored, or disrespected, our natural reaction is often immediate. We fight back. Become defensive. Withdraw. Send the message. Raise our voice. Or say something we later regret.

Jesus teaches a different way. “Love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you.” \- Matthew 5:44

Jesus is not teaching us to ignore hurt, tolerate abuse, or pretend that wrong behaviour is acceptable. He is teaching us that **someone else’s behaviour does not have to determine ours**.

Emotional maturity means creating space between **what I feel** and **what I do next**.

Your emotions provide information, but they do not have to give instructions.

This is where the Holy Spirit forms us.

Instead of immediately reacting from anger, fear, pride, or hurt, we can pause long enough to ask:

**“Holy Spirit, how would You have me respond?”**

Sometimes the Spirit may lead us to speak. Sometimes to wait. Sometimes to forgive. Sometimes to establish a boundary.

Formation happens when our responses gradually become less controlled by our impulses and more shaped by the character of Jesus.

## Practice

When you feel triggered today: STOP. Take one slow breath. Name what you feel.

## Reflection

What would a Spirit-led response look like instead?
