**John 15:4–5; Romans 15:13**

## Reading Notes

This Is Not the Finish Line

For 25 days, you have learned to notice, surrender, renew, grow, and flourish.

But emotional health does not mean always choosing ☺️ or 😍.

Jesus never promised a life without sadness, disappointment, anger, or difficult seasons. Instead, He invites us to: “Remain in me.” \- John 15:4

Flourishing means staying rooted in Jesus through every emotion and every season.

You have learned to recognise what is happening within you, surrender what you cannot control, renew unhealthy thinking, and allow the Holy Spirit to form Christlike responses.

Now, keep practising.

Some days you will feel strong. Other days you may struggle again. Growth is rarely a straight line.

When that happens, return to Jesus.

**The goal isn't emotional perfection.**

The goal is becoming more aware of God, more honest with yourself, and more like Jesus.
