## Today's Focus

What does Jesus commission his followers to do and what does he promise them as they go?

## Reading Notes

The final scene of Matthew's Gospel takes place on a mountain in Galilee, the region where the ministry began, the place Jesus had appointed. The eleven come, and Matthew records something that is easy to rush past: when they saw him, they worshipped him, but some doubted. The commission is given to a group that includes doubt, not after the doubt has been resolved, but within it. All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptising them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. The commission has a foundation: all authority. It has a scope: all nations. It has a method: making disciples, baptising, teaching. And it has a promise that closes the Gospel: I am with you always, to the end of the age. The study of Jesus that these 25 days have been is not an academic exercise. It has been preparation for this: to be sent, as Jesus was sent by the Father, into the world that God so loved. The new humanity is not assembled for its own benefit. It is formed in the image of Jesus in order to carry his presence into every corner of the world he died to redeem.

## Key Idea

_The commission is given on the basis of his authority, carried by his presence, directed toward all nations. The new humanity does not exist for itself; it is sent, as Jesus was sent, for the sake of the world._

## Reflection

After 25 days with Jesus, encountering who he is, how he lived, what he taught, and what he accomplished: what is the one truth that has taken the deepest root in your heart? And as you look ahead, how does his commission change where you go from here?

## Today's Practice

Gather with at least one other person from your community. Share what this study has done in you. Then pray Matthew 28:18-20 together, not as a formula but as a commissioning. Go from that prayer into your week as people who have been sent.
