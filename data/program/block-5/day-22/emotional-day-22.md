**Romans 5:3–5; Matthew 11:28–30**

## Reading Notes

Weakness is not failure. We often associate emotional health with being strong, calm, and having everything together.

But Jesus never asked us to pretend we are strong.

He said: “Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.” \- Matthew 11:28

Weakness can become the place where we learn dependence.

Romans 5 teaches that suffering can produce perseverance, character, and hope. This doesn't mean pain itself is good or that we should ignore difficult emotions. It means **God can form something good within us even through difficult seasons**.

Sometimes flourishing looks less like feeling powerful and more like continuing to trust God when you feel weak.

The Holy Spirit meets us there. Romans reminds us that God's love has been poured into our hearts through the Holy Spirit.

You don't need to hide your weakness from God.

Bring Him your exhaustion, disappointment, and limitations.

**Strength begins when we stop pretending we don't need help.**

## Practice

Name one area where you feel weak. Place your hand over your heart and pray: “Jesus, I need You here. Holy Spirit, strengthen me today.”

## Reflection

Where are you feeling emotionally tired?
