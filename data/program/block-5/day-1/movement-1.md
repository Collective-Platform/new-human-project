## MOVEMENT 1 — Who He Is

# The identity of Jesus — and the question he puts to every person

Days 1–7 · John 1 · Matthew 16 · Mark 1 · John 8 · Hebrews 1

---

Before we consider what Jesus did or what he taught, we must sit with who he is. The Gospels do not present Jesus as a great teacher who happened to be unusually close to God. They present him as the eternal Son of God who entered human history in a specific body, in a specific time, in a specific place and who makes claims about himself that no merely good teacher could or would make. These seven days move through the great identity passages of the New Testament, asking what each one reveals about who Jesus actually is and what that demands of those who encounter him.
