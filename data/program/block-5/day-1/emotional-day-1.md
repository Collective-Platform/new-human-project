**Mark 14:32–34**

**32** They went to a place called Gethsemane, and Jesus said to his disciples, “Sit here while I pray.” **33** He took Peter, James and John along with him, and he began to be deeply distressed and troubled. **34** “My soul is overwhelmed with sorrow to the point of death,” he said to them. “Stay here and keep watch.”

## Reading Notes

One of the most powerful pictures of Jesus' emotional life happens in the Garden of Gethsemane. Jesus knew the cross was coming. And He didn't pretend that everything was okay.

He told His disciples: "My soul is overwhelmed with sorrow to the point of death." Mark 14:34. Think about that. Jesus, the Son of God, could say: **"I am overwhelmed."**

He didn't hide His sorrow. He didn't pretend to be strong. He didn't tell Himself, *"I shouldn't feel this way because I trust God."*

He acknowledged what was happening within Him and brought it honestly before His Father. That teaches us something important about emotional health: **Feeling an emotion is not the same as being controlled by an emotion.**

You can feel afraid and still trust God. You can feel sad and still have faith. You can feel disappointed and still worship. You can feel overwhelmed and still know that God is with you.

Emotional maturity doesn't begin by suppressing what we feel. It begins by **noticing it**.

## Practice

Find a quiet place and take three slow breaths.

## Reflection

Are there emotions you tend to hide because you think Christians "shouldn't" feel them?
