## Reading Notes

**When Control Feels Like Safety** Most of us like to know what is going to happen.

We make plans. We prepare. We organise. We think ahead. None of these things are wrong. In fact, planning can be wise.

But somewhere along the way, planning can quietly become **control**.

Planning says: **“I will prepare wisely for tomorrow.”**

Control says: **“I need tomorrow to happen the way I expect; otherwise I cannot be okay.”**

And when things don't go according to plan, anxiety begins to rise. What if this doesn't work? What if they say no?

What if something goes wrong? What if I don't have enough?

What if my child makes the wrong decision? What if my future doesn't look the way I imagined?

We begin mentally rehearsing every possible outcome, hoping that if we think about it long enough, we can somehow prevent something bad from happening.

But there is a difficult truth we all eventually have to face: **You cannot control everything.**

And surprisingly, accepting that truth can become the beginning of peace.

## Practice

Take a piece of paper and draw two circles. In the first circle, write: WHAT I CAN DO, and in the second circle, write: WHAT I MUST SURRENDER.

## Reflection

Are you carrying responsibility for something—or someone—that God never asked you to control?
