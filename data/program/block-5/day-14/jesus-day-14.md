## Today's Focus

What is the Kingdom of God that Jesus announces and how does it change the way we see the world?

## Reading Notes

The first words of Jesus's public ministry in Mark are: The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand; repent and believe in the gospel. Everything Jesus does and teaches flows from this announcement. The kingdom of God is not a place; it is the active reign of God, breaking into history through Jesus. It is already present in the healings, the freedom, the forgiveness, and the welcome of sinners, yet not yet complete, awaiting its full arrival. Jesus describes it in parables that surprise the listener: it is like a mustard seed, the smallest thing that produces the largest result. It is like yeast hidden in flour, working invisibly through everything. It is like treasure in a field, like a pearl of great price, something worth selling everything to obtain. The kingdom does not arrive with visible fanfare. It is in your midst, present where Jesus is, growing from within, transforming what it touches before anyone notices. To repent and believe is to reorient your whole life around this reality: God's reign has broken into history, and nothing will ever be the same.

## Key Idea

_The Kingdom of God is not a future destination it is a present reality, breaking in wherever Jesus is. To believe the gospel is to reorient your whole life around that reality._

## Reflection

What difference does it make to your daily life that the Kingdom of God is already present and not only coming? Where do you see signs of it breaking through around you? Where do you need to look more carefully?

## Today's Practice

Read the three parables of Matthew 13:31-46 and ask: what is each one costing the person who finds the Kingdom? Write down what the Kingdom is currently costing you and what it is worth.
