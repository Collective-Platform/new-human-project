## MOVEMENT 3 — What He Taught

# The teaching of Jesus — and what it demands of those who hear it

Days 14–19 · Matthew 5–7 · Luke 15 · John 14–17

---

Jesus was more than a teacher, but he was not less than one. His teaching was so striking, surprising, and powerful that it produced two responses everywhere: life change or offence. There was no middle ground. This section moves through the main parts of his teaching: the Sermon on the Mount, the parables, the kingdom, the hard sayings, the command to love, and his final words in John 14 through 17\. The goal is not simply to understand these as ideas, but to let them do what they were designed to do: change how you live.
