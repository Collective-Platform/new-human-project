## Today's Focus

What changes when Paul says believers have died and their life is hidden with Christ?

## Reading Notes

Paul gives concrete commands. Put to death what is earthly: sexual immorality, impurity, passion, evil desire, and covetousness, which he identifies with idolatry. He also names anger, wrath, malice, slander, obscene talk, and lying. The reason is not merely that these behaviours are socially undesirable. He says the old self has been put off and the new self is being renewed in knowledge after the image of God. He then describes a community in which old social distinctions no longer determine identity because Christ is equally complete and enough for every believer.

## Key Idea

Colossians explicitly describes putting off the old self and putting on the new, renewed in the Creator’s image (3:9–11). Its account of identity across Greek/Jew and slave/free distinctions makes this a central passage for our New Human Project theme.

## Reflection

How does the new self in Colossians 3:9–11 relate to the one new humanity you studied in Ephesians 2:14–16? What is similar, and what is distinct?

Which 'old self' pattern does Paul name most directly in your life?

## Today's Practice

Choose one behaviour from the passage to put off today. Also identify the person or relationship most affected when you practice it.
