## Today's Focus

What does Paul mean by 'the mystery' that has now been revealed?

## Reading Notes

Paul describes the mystery as something hidden for ages and generations but now revealed to God's saints. This mystery is the fact that all believers, both Jew and Gentiles now possess the spirit of Christ in them and that is a token to future glory. His ministry is to proclaim Christ, warning and teaching everyone with wisdom so that everyone may be presented mature in Christ. He speaks of struggling and labouring with the strength Christ supplies.

## Key Idea

Paul's mystery is not a secret code reserved for a spiritual elite. It is something previously hidden that God has now made known, centred on Christ and his people.

## Reflection

What does Paul's description of ministry tell you about the relationship between proclamation and maturity?

## Today's Practice

Read Colossians 1:28 and ask yourself which areas of your life do you lack spiritual maturity. Pray and present it to God.
