## Today's Focus

Why did Paul confront Peter publicly at Antioch?

## Reading Notes

Paul says Peter had been eating with Gentiles, but withdrew when some Jews allegedly sent by James came. Peter did this out of fear of losing approval among the Jewish community. Paul's objection is not simply about table manners. Paul was upset because this behavior was not the love model of Christ they had been fighting for. A shared table fellowship signifies essentially whether Gentile and Jews are treated as full members of the people of God.

## Key Idea

The gospel’s truth is visible in sharing life together: Peter’s withdrawal from eating with Gentiles made belonging a practical question. This is one concrete test of A New Humanity.

## Reflection

Does your community avoid sharing life with people outside of the church sometimes? How so?

## Today's Practice

Observe one repeated practice in your church or community. Ask: 'What does this practice communicate about who belongs?'
