**Galatians 6:2 (NIV)**

Carry each other’s burdens, and in this way you will fulfill the law of Christ.

## Reading Notes

Healing rarely happens in isolation.

We were created for connection—not just casual connection, but safe, honest relationships.

When you share your burdens, something shifts. Shame loses power. Clarity increases.

You don’t need many people. You need **safe people**.

## Practice

Open up to someone you trust.

## Reflection

Who can I be real with?
