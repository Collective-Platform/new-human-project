## Today's Focus

How does Paul describe a Spirit-shaped community?

## Reading Notes

Paul turns from the fruit of the Spirit to concrete relationships. If someone is caught in a transgression, the spiritual person should restore them gently, while keeping watch over themselves. Believers are to bear one another's burdens, test their own work, share with those who teach, and not grow weary in doing good. Paul warns against boasting in converting people using the law, instead he describes the cross as the basis of his own boasting. Paul explains that believers will experience peace and mercy as a result of being under a new creation.

## Key Idea

Life by the Spirit becomes visible in restoration, humility, burden-bearing, perseverance, generosity, and the refusal to boast in human credentials.

## Reflection

Which part of Galatians 6 is the most challenging to practice in your community?

## Today's Practice

Do one hidden act of burden-bearing today. Help someone without announcing that you helped.
