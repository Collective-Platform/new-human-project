**Proverbs 3:5–6 (NIV)**

Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways submit to him, and he will make your paths straight.

## Reading Notes

Control feels safe—but it creates anxiety.

Because you were never meant to carry everything.

**Surrender is not weakness. It is trust.**

Letting go does not mean you stop caring. It means you trust God more than your need to control.

## Practice

Surrender one worry.

## Reflection

What am I holding too tightly?
