## Today's Focus

How does Paul apply the new life in Christ to household relationships?

## Reading Notes

Paul addresses wives and husbands, children and fathers, and slaves and masters. He gives specific instructions to each group, repeatedly placing conduct under the authority of the Lord. Wives are told to submit to husbands as is fitting in the Lord; husbands are told to love and not be harsh; children are told to obey parents; fathers are warned not to provoke children; slaves are told to obey earthly masters sincerely and to work heartily for the Lord; masters are told to treat slaves justly and fairly, remembering that they too have a Master in heaven.

## Key Idea

The New Human Project reaches ordinary relationships, but Colossians addresses ancient household structures, including slavery. Read its instructions in their historical setting, attending carefully to what Paul says to each group rather than treating those structures as a timeless ideal.

## Reflection

What can you learn from what Paul teaches about slaves and masters? How would you apply those teachings in your workplace today?

## Today's Practice

Choose the relationship closest to your daily life. Write one responsibility the passage places on you toward the other person. Pray and ask the Holy Spirit to guide you in carrying out that responsibility well.
