## Today's Focus

Why does Paul move from warnings about religious regulations to the command to seek things above?

## Reading Notes

Paul says that if believers have died with Christ to the elemental principles of the world, they should not submit to regulations such as 'do not handle, do not taste, do not touch.' These man made rules concern things that perish with use and are of no value to our spiritual life. Paul tells them to set their minds on things above rather than on earthly things. Their life is described as hidden or having complete exclusive access in God, with the promise that Christ will appear when He returns and they will appear with him in glory.

## Key Idea

Paul's answer to legalistic or merely external religion is not indifference but a deeper reorientation around Christ.

## Reflection

What does 'seek the things above' mean when read in the immediate context of Colossians 2–3?

## Today's Practice

Take ten minutes without your phone. Read Colossians 3:1-4 and identify one thing that usually distracts you from setting your gaze on Jesus.
