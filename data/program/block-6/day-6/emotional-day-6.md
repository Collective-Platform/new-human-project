**1 Corinthians 6:19-20 (NIV)**

Do you not know that your bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit, who is in you, whom you have received from God? You are not your own; you were bought at a price. Therefore, honour God with your bodies.

## Reading Notes

Your body often speaks before your mind understands.

Tight shoulders. Heavy breathing. Fatigue. These are not just physical—they are emotional signals.

**God designed you as a whole being. Your body, mind, and spirit are connected.**

To ignore your body is to ignore part of how God is communicating with you.

## Practice

Notice where your body holds tension.

## Reflection

What is my body trying to tell me?
