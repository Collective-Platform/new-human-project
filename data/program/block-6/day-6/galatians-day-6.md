## Today's Focus

Why is Paul so uncompromising about the gospel?

## Reading Notes

Across the opening chapters, Paul's urgency becomes understandable. The issue is not a minor disagreement about religious style. The gospel concerns Christ's self-sacrifice, the basis of justification, the place of Gentiles in the eyes of God, and the impact of the cross on humanity. Paul therefore uses unusually severe language for any message or attempt that alters the gospel he received and preached.

## Key Idea

Paul's intensity comes from what he believes is at stake: the truth of the gospel and the freedom of those who depend on it.

## Reflection

What core truth of the gospel do you think is easiest for Christians to distort or dilute?

Write a one-liner of the gospel using only ideas and language you can support directly from Galatians 1–2.
