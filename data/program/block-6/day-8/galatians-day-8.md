## Today's Focus

What role does Paul give to the promise to Abraham and to the law?

## Reading Notes

Paul argues that a human covenant once confirmed, is irrevocable, what more a Godly covenant made with God. Paul says the covenant with Abraham is more superior than the law because the covenant is part of the entire salvation promise from God to the world while the law is just a mirror that reveals our sin but does not save. With faith having come, believers are described as sons of God through faith in Christ Jesus and as Abraham's offspring and heirs according to promise.

## Key Idea

Paul distinguishes promise and law, then says those who belong to Christ are children of God regardless of social statuses and are instantly heirs according to the promise (3:26–29). This is a direct Galatians connection to our Ephesians theme of A New Humanity.

## Reflection

How does Galatians 3:26–29 deepen or challenge your understanding of who belongs in the New Human Project?

What questions does this passage leave you with about promise, law, faith, and maturity?

How would you view people in church who are different from you now that you know God places equal value on all His children?
