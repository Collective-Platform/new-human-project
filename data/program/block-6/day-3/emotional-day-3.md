**John 8:32 (NIV)**

Then you will know the truth, and the truth will set you free.’

## Reading Notes

Confusion keeps you stuck. Clarity moves you forward.

Many people live with vague emotional language:  
“I’m just off.”  
“I’m just tired.”

But beneath those words are deeper truths waiting to be named.

When you name something clearly, you bring it into the light. And what is in the light can be healed.

Freedom does not begin with change. It begins with truth.
