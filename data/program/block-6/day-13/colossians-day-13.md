## Today's Focus

What does Paul thank God for when he thinks about the Colossian believers?

## Reading Notes

Paul gives thanks for their faith in Christ Jesus and the proof of their faith, which is the love for all believers, grounded in the hope laid up for them in heaven. He connects this with the gospel, which is bearing fruit and increasing throughout the world. He then prays that they will be filled with the knowledge of God's will in spiritual wisdom and understanding, so that they may walk in a manner worthy of the Lord, bearing fruit, growing in knowledge, being strengthened, and giving thanks.

## Key Idea

Paul connects gospel truth with a pattern of faith, love, hope, growth, endurance, and thanksgiving.

## Reflection

Which of faith, love, hope, growth, endurance, and thanksgiving most needs attention in your life?

## Today's Practice

Pray Colossians 1:9-14 for yourself or someone in your community, using the text's own categories.
