**Proverbs 25:28 (NIV)**

Like a city whose walls are broken through is a person who lacks self-control.

## Reading Notes

Without boundaries, your emotional world becomes crowded.

You say yes when you should say no. You carry what was never yours to carry.

**Boundaries are not selfish. They are stewards.**

They protect your peace so you can love well.

## Practice

Set one boundary today.

## Reflection

Where am I overextending?
