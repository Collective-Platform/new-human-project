## Today's Focus

What does Paul's closing list of people reveal about the nature of his ministry?

## Reading Notes

Paul names Tychicus, Onesimus, Aristarchus, Mark, Justus, Epaphras, Luke, Demas, Nympha, Archippus, and others. Some are described as beloved fellow servants, fellow workers, or people who labour in prayer. The closing is not an impersonal administrative appendix. It shows a ministry network made up of people with different histories, roles, and relationships.

## Key Idea

The New Human Project is not carried by isolated individuals: Paul’s closing greetings show a network of named coworkers, households, prayer, and mutual encouragement.

## Reflection

Which people have quietly made your ministry or work possible?

## Today's Practice

Make a list of ten people who have contributed to your life or calling. Thank at least two of them personally.
