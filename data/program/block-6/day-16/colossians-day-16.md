## Today's Focus

What does Paul mean by being rooted, built up, and made alive in Christ?

## Reading Notes

Paul tells the Colossians to walk in Christ as they received him, rooted and built up in him and established in the faith. He warns against being deceived by false teachers and empty deceit. These teachings were according to human tradition and elemental principles rather than Christ. He then says the fullness of deity dwells bodily in Christ, stressing that Jesus was not only fully God but fully human as well. The passage connects baptismal imagery, burial and resurrection with Christ, forgiveness of trespasses, and God's action in making believers alive with him.

## Key Idea

Paul's response to competing teachings is not to add something alongside Christ but to insist on the fullness believers already have in Christ.

## Reflection

What voices or practices may pose a distraction in your faith with Christ?

## Today's Practice

Pray and ask God to help you as you continue to grow and mature in Christ. Ask for wisdom to not be easily distracted by unsound doctrine.
