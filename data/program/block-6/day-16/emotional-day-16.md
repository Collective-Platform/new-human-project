**Zechariah 4:10 (NIV)**

‘Who dares despise the day of small things, since the seven eyes of the LORD that range throughout the earth will rejoice when they see the chosen capstone in the hand of Zerubbabel?’

## Reading Notes

Growth is often slow—and easy to overlook.

We wait for big breakthroughs, but ignore small progress.

Yet **small wins matter**.

They are evidence that something is changing.

Emotionally healthy people don’t wait for perfection. They celebrate movement.

## Practice

Acknowledge one small win.

## Reflection

What has improved?
