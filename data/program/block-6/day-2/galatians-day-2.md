## Today's Focus

How does Paul describe the change from his former life to his calling in Christ?

## Reading Notes

Paul recalls his former conduct in Judaism, where he violently persecuted the church and attempted to destroy it. He acknowledges that God through His grace revealed Jesus to him so he could preach the gospel to the Gentiles. Paul emphasizes that he did not consult anyone else when he started preaching about Jesus, not even those who were more experienced or qualified than him in Jerusalem. Everything he preached was from the revelation he received from Christ. He only visited Jerusalem and other cities later after three years of being prepared for ministry by God in the deserts of Arabia.

## Key Idea

Paul interprets his life as a movement from passion directed against the church to a grace-given calling directed toward the Gentiles.

## Reflection

How does Paul distinguish between passion for inherited convictions and faithfulness to the revelation of Christ?

## Today's Practice

Recall one major reorientation Christ has produced in your own life. Give thanks for His grace that orchestrated that incident.
