**Psalm 62:8 (NIV)**

Trust in him at all times, you people; pour out your hearts to him, for God is our refuge.

## Reading Notes

Many of us have learned to edit our emotions before bringing them to God.

We bring the “acceptable” feelings—but hide the messy ones.

Yet Scripture invites us to **pour out our hearts**.

God is not looking for polished prayers. He is looking for honest ones.

Even Jesus wept. Even Jesus felt anguish.

**Emotions are not a sign of weakness—they are a sign that you are human.**

And your humanity is not a barrier to God. It is where He meets you.

## Practice

Journal honestly without filtering.

## Reflection

What am I afraid to feel?
