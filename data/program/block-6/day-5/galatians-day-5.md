## Today's Focus

What does Paul mean when he says that a person is justified through faith in Christ and not by works of the law?

## Reading Notes

Paul argues from his perspective as a Jew that even Jewish believers know that a person is not justified by works of the law but through faith in Jesus Christ. Three times, he emphasizes the word “justified” in the same verse, highlighting the law cannot save but only Christ can. He then describes his own life in relation to Christ: his old self has died, his sins crucified with Christ, now he is born again and Christ lives in him. Paul closes with a crucial statement: if righteousness could come through the law, Christ died for no purpose.

## Key Idea

Paul places Christ's death and the believer's new life at the centre of his argument, rejecting any basis of righteousness that would make Christ's death unnecessary.

## Reflection

What does your life reveal about where you believe your standing before God comes from?
