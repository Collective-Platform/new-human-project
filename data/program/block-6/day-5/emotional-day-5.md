**Proverbs 4:23 (NIV)**

Above all else, guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it.

## Reading Notes

Your reactions are often rooted in something deeper than the moment.

A simple comment feels heavy.  
A small situation feels overwhelming.

Why? Because it touched something already sensitive inside you.

**Your triggers are not random. They are revealing.**

They show you where healing is still needed— not to shame you, but to guide you.

## Practice

Identify one trigger today.

## Reflection

What did this situation remind me of?
