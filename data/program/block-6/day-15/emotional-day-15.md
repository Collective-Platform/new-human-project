**John 14:26 (NIV)**

But the Advocate, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, will teach you all things and will remind you of everything I have said to you.

## Reading Notes

You are not called to navigate your emotions alone.

The Holy Spirit is present—not just to comfort you, but to guide you into truth.

He gently convicts.  
He reminds.  
He leads.

When you invite Him in, your emotional world becomes a place of transformation, not confusion.

## Practice

Invite the Holy Spirit into your emotions.

## Reflection

What is He showing me right now?
