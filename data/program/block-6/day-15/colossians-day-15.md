## Today's Focus

What does Paul say Christ's death accomplishes for people who were once alienated from God?

## Reading Notes

Paul describes the Colossians as once alienated and hostile in mind, doing evil deeds. He says Christ has now reconciled them in his body of flesh by his death, in order to present them holy, blameless, and above reproach. He connects this with continuing in the faith, stable and steadfast, not shifting from the hope of the gospel. Paul then describes his ministry as suffering for the sake of Christ's body, proclaiming Christ, warning and teaching everyone with wisdom, with the goal of presenting everyone mature in Christ.

## Key Idea

Reconciliation, perseverance, proclamation, suffering, and maturity are held together in Paul's description of ministry.

## Reflection

How does Paul connect what Christ has done with the way believers are expected to continue?

## Today's Practice

Pray for one person you are helping spiritually. Ask specifically for growth toward maturity rather than merely comfort or success.
