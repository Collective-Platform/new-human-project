**2 Corinthians 5:17 (NIV)**

Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: the old has gone, the new is here\!

## Reading Notes

**Emotions are real—but they are not your identity.**

You may feel anxious, but you are not defined by anxiety.

You may feel rejected, but you are not rejected.

When identity is unclear, emotions take over. But when identity is anchored in Christ, emotions lose their authority.

You feel them—but you are not ruled by them.

## Practice

Declare truth over yourself.

## Reflection

Who am I in Christ today?
