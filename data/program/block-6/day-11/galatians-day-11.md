## Today's Focus

What does Paul mean by walking by the Spirit?

## Reading Notes

Paul contrasts the desires of the flesh with the desires of the Spirit. He lists works of the flesh and then contrasts them with the fruit of the Spirit: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control. He says those who belong to Christ have crucified the flesh with its passions and desires. The practical conclusion is direct: if we live by the Spirit, let us also keep in step with the Spirit, refusing conceit, provocation, and envy.

## Key Idea

The Spirit-shaped life is described not merely by what it rejects but by the character it produces.

## Reflection

Which fruit is most visible in your life right now, and which needs deliberate cultivation?

## Today's Practice

Choose one fruit of the Spirit that you are struggling with. Pray and ask God to help you with it today.
