## Today's Focus

What does Paul say about the source and authority of the gospel he preaches?

## Reading Notes

Paul opens unusually sharp. He identifies himself as an apostle and immediately announces grace and peace from God and Christ, who gave himself for sins to deliver believers from the present evil age. He then expresses astonishment that the Galatians are turning to a different gospel. Paul insists that the gospel he preached was not according to man and that he did not receive it from a human source or learn it as a human tradition. His language is deliberately strong: even if an angel were to proclaim a different gospel, it must be rejected.

## Key Idea

Paul treats the gospel as a message whose authority does not rest on its popularity, novelty, or the status of its messenger.

## Reflection

What sources most strongly shape your understanding of the gospel: Scripture, tradition, culture, personalities, experience, or something else?

## Today's Practice

Read Galatians 1:6-9 twice. Write down exactly what Paul says makes a message 'another gospel.'
