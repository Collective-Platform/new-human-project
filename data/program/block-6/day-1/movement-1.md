## MOVEMENT 1 — The Gospel of Grace

# Paul’s defence of the gospel asks a foundational question for the New Human Project: on what basis do people belong as God’s people?

Days 1–6 · Galatians 1–2

---

Galatians opens with urgency. Paul defends the gospel he proclaimed and explains why lacking understanding in the gospel message can be detrimental to not only the individual but the community as well. As we continue from Ephesians, notice especially how gospel truth affects how we should approach hospitality, shared meals, and fostering a sense of belonging (Galatians 2:11–14). The theme of A New Humanity emerges from these concrete concerns, not from a slogan added to the text.
