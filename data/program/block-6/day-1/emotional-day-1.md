**Matthew 6:34**

Therefore do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will worry about itself. Each day has enough trouble of its own.

## Reading Notes

One of the greatest challenges of our generation is not necessarily sin. **It is a distraction**.

Many of us are physically present but mentally absent. We replay conversations from yesterday. We worry about problems that have not yet arrived. We carry tomorrow's burdens before tomorrow ever comes. As a result, we miss the grace available today.

Jesus repeatedly taught people to trust the Father one day at a time.

Notice that He never promised answers for every future concern.

Instead, He promised daily bread. Daily grace. Daily strength.

The problem is that we often try to fight tomorrow's battles with today's resources. No wonder we feel overwhelmed.

When anxiety grows, it is often because our minds have moved ahead of where God is currently providing grace.

The present moment is where God meets us.

The present moment is where God speaks.

The present moment is where obedience happens.

Learning to be present does not mean ignoring the future. It means refusing to live there.

A healthy emotional life is built by learning to receive today as a gift. Not yesterday. Not tomorrow. **TODAY**.

## Practice

Three times today, stop and take three slow breaths. Notice where your mind has wandered. Gently bring your attention back to God and the present moment.

## Reflection

How can I trust God with it today?
