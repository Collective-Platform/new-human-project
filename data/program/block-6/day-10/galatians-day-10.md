## Today's Focus

What does Paul mean by Christian freedom?

## Reading Notes

Paul begins with a direct command: stand firm and do not submit again to a yoke of slavery. He warns that accepting circumcision as a requirement for justification would place the person under obligation to keep the whole law and would sever the logic of relying on Christ's grace. Yet Paul immediately warns believers from manipulating the freedom they have received. With this freedom, believers are called to serve one another through love, not for selfish desires. Paul also gives a graphic picture of what happens when believers do not act in love and serve each other: biting and devouring one another.

## Key Idea

Paul holds two truths together: freedom from the old life of slavery and freedom for loving service.

## Reflection

How can freedom become self-centred? How does Paul redirect freedom toward other people?

## Today's Practice

Choose one person you normally serve only when convenient. Do one deliberate act of service without seeking recognition.
