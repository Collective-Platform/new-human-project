**Psalm 139:23–24; Galatians 6:9**

## Reading Notes

For 25 days, you have paused to notice your emotions and bring them before God. Your mood record may reveal joyful days, difficult moments, or feelings that keep returning. Each entry offers an opportunity to understand what is happening within you.

As you look back, approach yourself with compassion. Emotional growth does not mean feeling happy every day. Growth may look like recognising anxiety sooner, naming hurt honestly, pausing before reacting, or asking someone for help.

In Psalm 139, David invites God to search his heart and lead him. We can bring that same openness to our emotions. Ask the Holy Spirit to help you recognise what needs attention, surrender what you cannot control, and practise responses that give life.

**Galatians 6:9** encourages us to persevere in doing good. Some changes take time. Keep making room for prayer, rest, honest conversations, and healthy boundaries.

These 25 days are a beginning. Carry forward the awareness you have gained, trusting God to continue shaping how you feel, respond, and relate to others.

## Practice

Review your mood entries. Identify one recurring emotion, one situation that affected your mood, and one practice that helped you. Thank God for one sign of growth, then choose one habit to continue for the next month.

## Reflection

1. What have I learned about my emotions over these 25 days?
2. Where do I notice growth in how I respond?
