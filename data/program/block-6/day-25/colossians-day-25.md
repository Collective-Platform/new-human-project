## Today's Focus

After twenty-five days in Galatians and Colossians, what does Paul place at the centre of the Christian life?

## Reading Notes

Galatians conclude with the cross, new creation, and grace (6:14–18). Colossians centres life on Christ and describes a new self renewed in the Creator’s image (3:1–11). Alongside Ephesians’ “one new humanity” (2:15), these are complementary but distinct ways Paul speaks about the people formed in Christ. Our church’s New Human Project theme draws them together without claiming that all three letters use identical language.

## Key Idea

The New Human Project is grounded in Christ: grace, faith, the Spirit, new creation, and a renewed shared life are connected themes across these letters, each expressed in its own context.

## Reflection

What has Galatians or Colossians added to your understanding of A New Humanity beyond what you learned in Ephesians?

## Today's Practice

Write a one-page response under three headings: “What Christ has done,” “Who we are together,” and “How we will live.” Ground each section in at least one passage from Galatians or Colossians.
