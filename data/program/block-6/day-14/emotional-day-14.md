**Ephesians 4:31–32 (NIV)**

Get rid of all bitterness, rage and anger, brawling and slander, along with every form of malice. Be kind and compassionate to one another, forgiving each other, just as in Christ God forgave you.

## Reading Notes

Unforgiveness keeps you tied to the pain of the past.

It replays the moment again and again. It keeps the wound open.

**Forgiveness does not mean what happened was okay. It means you are choosing not to carry it anymore.**

It is not instant. It is a journey.

But every step toward forgiveness is a step toward freedom.

## Practice

Release one offense to God.

## Reflection

What am I still holding onto?
