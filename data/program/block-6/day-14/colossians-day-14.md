## Today's Focus

What does Paul say about the identity and position of Christ in creation?

## Reading Notes

Paul describes Christ as the perfect image of the invisible God and the firstborn of all creation. This tells us that Jesus is fully God in every way. He then strengthens his point by giving more reasons: all things were created through him and for him; he is before all things; in him all things hold together. Christ is also described as head of the body, the church, the beginning, the firstborn from the dead, and the one in whom all the fullness was pleased to dwell. Through him God reconciles man and all things, through what was done on the cross.

## Key Idea

Paul's description places Christ in relation to creation, the church, resurrection, fullness, and reconciliation.

## Reflection

How does Colossians 1:15-20 give you a better understanding of why Jesus’s death was important for the restoration of our relationship with God?

## Today's Practice

Read Colossians 1:15-20 aloud. Underline every statement about Christ. Spend the rest of the prayer simply responding to what the passage says.
