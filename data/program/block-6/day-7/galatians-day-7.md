## Today's Focus

How does Paul connect the dots between God’s promise to His people and receiving the Spirit through faith and through the life of Abraham?

## Reading Notes

Paul asks the Galatians whether they received the Spirit by works of the law or by hearing with faith. He points to Abraham, who believed God and was counted as righteous. Paul explains that salvation has always been only through faith, citing the story of Abraham as an example. He also cites Scripture's language about blessing and the curse associated with failing to abide by the law, and argues that Christ redeemed believers from the curse of the law so that the blessing of Abraham might come to the Gentiles and that believers might receive the promised Spirit through faith.

## Key Idea

Paul's argument connects faith, Abraham, Christ, blessing, and the Spirit within one story of promise.

## Reflection

What connections does Paul himself make between Abraham's faith and the experience of believers in Christ?

## Today's Practice

Trace Paul's argument in Galatians 3:6-14 using arrows: Abraham → faith → promise → Christ → blessing → Spirit.
