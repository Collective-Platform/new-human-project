**Matthew 11:28 (NIV)**

‘Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.

## Reading Notes

Not everything needs to be solved. Some things need to be rested.

We often push through exhaustion, thinking it is strength. But constant exhaustion dulls your soul.

**Jesus invites the weary, not the strong.**

**Rest is not quitting.** It is trusting that God can sustain you even when you stop.

## Practice

Take intentional rest.

## Reflection

Am I tired or truly overwhelmed?
