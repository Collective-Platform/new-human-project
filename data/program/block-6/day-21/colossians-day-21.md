## Today's Focus

What does Paul say the new life should look like inside a Christian community?

## Reading Notes

Paul tells the Colossians to put on compassionate hearts, kindness, humility, meekness, and patience. They are to bear with one another and forgive each other as the Lord has forgiven them. Above all, they are to put on love, which binds everything together in perfect harmony. The peace of Christ is to rule in their hearts, they are to be thankful, and the word of Christ is to dwell among them richly as they teach and admonish one another in wisdom. Whatever they do in word or deed is to be done in the name of the Lord Jesus.

## Key Idea

The New Human Project becomes visible in shared practices: compassion, forgiveness, love, peace, worship, and gratitude. These are the qualities Paul names for life together in Christ.

## Reflection

Which quality in this passage would most change the atmosphere of your relationships if it became more visible?

## Today's Practice

Choose one quality — patience, forgiveness, humility, kindness, or gratitude — and deliberately practice it in one difficult relationship.
