## Today's Focus

What does Paul mean by moving from slavery to sonship?

## Reading Notes

Paul uses the analogy of a child who, while underage, differs little in status from a slave because he is under guardians and managers until the appointed date. He then applies the analogy to a believer’s life where we are children or slaves before salvation (where believers are still enslaved by the basic things of the world) and adopted as sons or adults after salvation. Paul says God sent the Spirit of his Son into believers' hearts, crying 'Abba\! Father\!'

## Key Idea

Paul highlights the contrast of how the Christian identity is developed from being slaves and children spiritually to being adopted by a loving Father as sons and growing up as spiritual adults and also heirs.

## Reflection

What difference does Paul make between relating to God as a slave and relating to God as a child and heir?

## Today's Practice

Pray the short cry Paul gives: 'Abba\! Father\!' Then sit in silence for several minutes without adding a request.
