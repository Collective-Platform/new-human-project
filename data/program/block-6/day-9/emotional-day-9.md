**Romans 12:2 (NIV)**

Do not conform to the pattern of this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind. Then you will be able to test and approve what God’s will is \- his good, pleasing and perfect will.

## Reading Notes

Your thoughts shape your emotional world.

Left unchecked, negative thoughts become emotional patterns. And emotional patterns become your reality.

But not every thought deserves agreement.

God invites you to renew your mind—to align your thoughts with His truth.

**Because when your thinking changes, your emotional world begins to shift.**

## Practice

Replace one negative thought with truth.

## Reflection

What lies have I been believing?
