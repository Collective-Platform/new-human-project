## Today's Focus

What kinds of religious practices does Paul warn the Colossians not to let others use as a basis for judgment?

## Reading Notes

Paul names food and drink, festivals, new moons, and Sabbaths, describing them as a shadow of things to come while the substance belongs to Christ. He also warns about those who insist on ascetic practices, humility, and worship of angels, describing such approaches as involving visions and humanly generated regulations. Paul argues that these things have an appearance of wisdom but cannot restrain the indulgences of the flesh. His central concern is that believers hold fast to the Head, Christ.

## Key Idea

Paul distinguishes between practices that can look spiritually impressive and the reality of holding fast to Christ.

## Reflection

How can religious appearance become confused with spiritual maturity?

## Today's Practice

Ask where you personally may measure spirituality by outward appearances or practices rather than by attachment to Christ.
