## Today's Focus

What does Paul ask the church to pray for, and how should believers conduct themselves toward outsiders?

## Reading Notes

Paul calls the Colossians to continue steadfastly in prayer, staying awake and alert about what to pray rather than being vague. He asks them to pray for an opportunity so that he may declare the mystery of Christ. He also asks for wisdom in how they should answer each person. Their speech is to be gracious and seasoned with salt or purifying in a way so that others may know how to act in response to it.

## Key Idea

Paul tries to explain that the mission of evangelism must be done prayerfully, trusting in God to provide opportunities, and the spirit to provide wisdom and grace in addressing others.

## Reflection

Do you think a witness of God is someone who always has the right answers or someone who has wisdom in knowing what and when to say something?

## Today's Practice

Before your next significant conversation with someone outside your faith community, pray Colossians 4:5-6 over the interaction.
