## Today's Focus

_Why does Paul turn from the immoral pagan to the moral judge and what does that reveal?_

## Reading Notes

Just when a moral reader might nod along at Paul’s rebuke of wickedness, he turns the argument on them: you have no excuse when you judge others. The person who condemns immorality while practicing the same things is without defense. God’s judgment is based on truth, not outward appearances. He shows no favoritism and looks directly at the heart. Resting on your clean record, religious heritage, or correct theology will not save you. God’s judgment exposes what is done in secret and reveals our true motives. The good news is not only for the broken. It is equally urgent for the respectable and self righteous. True faith stops measuring itself against other people and stands honest before God

## Key Idea

The moral and religious person needs the gospel just as urgently as the obviously immoral. God judges the heart, not the reputation.

## Reflection

Where are you tempted to rely on your moral track record or church habits instead of God’s grace?

## Today's Practice

Take five minutes today for honest self examination. The goal is clarity, not guilt. Ask yourself: what am I secretly trusting besides Christ to make me acceptable to God? Name it out loud, and release it to Him in prayer.
