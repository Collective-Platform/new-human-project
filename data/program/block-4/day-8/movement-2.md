## MOVEMENT 2 — The Life We Have Been Given

# Peace, freedom, and the new humanity's life in Christ

Days 8–14 · Romans 5–8

---

Chapters 5–8 are the summit of Romans, and perhaps the summit of all Paul's writing. Having established that every human being is justified freely by grace through faith, Paul now unfolds what that justified life actually looks like from the inside. It is a life of peace with God, freedom from sin's power, dying to the old self and resurrection with Christ. It means walking not in the flesh but in the Spirit. These seven days are not primarily about doctrine to be understood, but about a life to enter. The greatest of these chapters, Romans 8, is one of the most beautiful and hope filled passages in all of Scripture.
