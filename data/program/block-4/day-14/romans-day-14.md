## Today's Focus

_How does Paul's vision of future glory transform the way the new humanity endures present suffering?_

## Reading Notes

‘The sufferings of this present time are not worth comparing with the glory that is to be revealed to us.’ Paul holds together present groaning and future glory with extraordinary confidence. The whole creation groans. We groan inwardly. Even the Spirit groans with us. But this groaning is not despair, for it is the groaning of a woman in labour: painful, purposeful, moving towards new life. And then Paul reaches his great conclusion, one of the most magnificent passages in Scripture: ‘If God is for us, who can be against us? He who did not spare his own Son but gave him up for us all, how will he not also with him graciously give us all things?’ Nothing in all creation, nothing in the entire universe, no power, no height, no depth, no element present or future, can separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus our Lord. The new humanity lives towards this. It is held by this. And it is freed by this to endure whatever comes.

## Key Idea

Nothing in all creation can separate you from the love of God in Christ Jesus. Not suffering, not failure, not death, not the future. Nothing. The new humanity lives from that security.

## Reflection

What is the thing you most fear could separate you from God: your failures, your doubts, your circumstances, or your past? Bring that specific fear to Romans 8 verse 38 to 39, and let his love answer it.

## Today's Practice

Read Romans 8:38–39 as a declaration over your specific fears. Write down the list of things Paul says cannot separate you from God's love. Then add below: 'Not \[your specific fear\] either.' Pray it as worship.
