## Today's Focus

_What does God's dealing with Israel in Romans 11 reveal about how God always works?_

## Reading Notes

Has God rejected his people? By no means. Paul's answer to his own question is immediate and personal: “I myself am an Israelite, a descendant of Abraham.” God has not abandoned Israel. He has preserved a remnant chosen by grace, just as in Elijah's time, when the prophet thought he was alone and seven thousand had not bowed the knee to Baal. Paul then introduces his image of the olive tree: Israel is the cultivated tree, and Gentile believers are wild branches grafted in. And here Paul issues his famous warning to Gentile Christians: “Do not be arrogant toward the natural branches.” If God did not spare the natural branches when they fell through unbelief, neither will he spare you if you become complacent. The grace that includes you is the same grace that can be withdrawn from the presumptuous. The new humanity holds no grounds for superiority over anyone.

## Key Idea

The grace that includes you is not your achievement. You are simply a branch grafted onto the tree, sustained by the same faith that has always held the people of God. There is no room for pride.

## Reflection

In what ways does your church or tradition harbour a sense of superiority, whether over other churches, non Christians, or Israel? What does the image of the olive tree say to that attitude today?

## Today's Practice

Pray specifically for the Jewish people today, giving thanks for the faithfulness of God to his covenant promises. Let your prayer be an act of gratitude for the ancient root into which you have been grafted.
