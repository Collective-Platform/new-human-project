## Today's Focus

_How does the cross of Christ address the human condition that Romans 1–3 has described?_

## Reading Notes

“But now”. Two of the most important words in the Bible. After three chapters of diagnosis, the solution arrives, and it is not what anyone expected. The righteousness of God has been revealed completely apart from the law, even though the Law and the Prophets point toward it. It is a righteousness given as a free gift. It is received through faith in Jesus Christ and is available to everyone who believes, with no exceptions. Paul then gives the most concentrated explanation of Jesus' death in all of Scripture: God put forward Jesus as the sacrifice that turns away wrath through His blood. The cross is not an accident. It is God's own act, sending His own Son to absorb the full weight of human sin. In doing this, God shows His perfect justice and declares righteous anyone who trusts in Jesus. Three chapters of bad news arrive at six verses of the most concentrated good news ever written. This is the hinge on which the whole letter turns.

## Key Idea

Righteousness before God is not achieved, it is received. God gives it as a gift through Christ's death, available equally to every human being who trusts him.

## Reflection

How deeply have you accepted the truth that your standing before God is a free gift rather than something you earn? What difference does this make to how you feel when you approach Him?

## Today's Practice

Pray Romans 3:23–24 as your own testimony today: “I have fallen short of your glory. I am justified freely by your grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus.” Say it slowly. Let it settle.
