**Scripture:** Romans 7:15–25 \- **15** I do not understand what I do. For what I want to do I do not do, but what I hate I do. **16** And if I do what I do not want to do, I agree that the law is good. **17** As it is, it is no longer I myself who do it, but it is sin living in me. **18** For I know that good itself does not dwell in me, that is, in my sinful nature.\[[a](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans%207%3A15-25&version=NIV#fen-NIV-28110a)\] For I have the desire to do what is good, but I cannot carry it out. **19** For **I do not do the good I want to do**, but the evil I do not want to do—this I keep on doing. **20** Now if I do what I do not want to do, it is no longer I who do it, but it is sin living in me that does it. **21** So I find this law at work: Although I want to do good, evil is right there with me. **22** For in my inner being I delight in God’s law; **23** but I see another law at work in me, waging war against the law of my mind and making me a prisoner of the law of sin at work within me. **24** What a wretched man I am\! Who will rescue me from this body that is subject to death? **25** Thanks be to God, who delivers me through Jesus Christ our Lord\! So then, I myself in my mind am a slave to God’s law, but in my sinful nature\[[b](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans%207%3A15-25&version=NIV#fen-NIV-28117b)\] a slave to the law of sin.

Have you ever reacted in a way that surprised yourself?  
Paul understood that struggle. "I do not do the good I want to do..." Even mature believers wrestle with unhealthy patterns. Emotional health begins when we stop pretending we have no struggles.

Instead of feeling condemned, Romans teaches us to become curious.  
Why do I react like this? What fear is underneath? What wound is influencing this response?

The Holy Spirit doesn't expose our hearts to shame us. He reveals them so He can transform them.

Conviction always leads us closer to Jesus. Shame pushes us further away. **Choose conviction.**

## Practice

Think about one emotional reaction you've had recently.

## Reflection

What recurring emotional pattern do you notice?
