## Today's Focus

_What is the vision Paul holds out for the new humanity at the end of his argument and what sustains it?_

## Reading Notes

May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, so that by the power of the Holy Spirit you may abound in hope. Paul is writing to a church that is divided, under pressure, living in the capital of an empire that will eventually persecute them. And his prayer for them is not survival or doctrinal uniformity. It is hope: overflowing, abounding hope, produced by the Holy Spirit in those who believe. The Gentiles have been included in the praise of God. The root of Jesse has come and the nations hope in him. The vision is cosmic: every nation, every people, Jew and Gentile together, glorifying God. The new humanity is not a small religious club. It is the firstfruits of a renewed world, a world in which the nations hope in Christ, and in which the God of hope is filling his people with joy and peace and overflowing hope by the power of his Spirit.

## Key Idea

The new humanity lives from hope: not optimism, not wishful thinking, but the overflowing, Spirit produced confidence that the God of hope is doing something the world has not yet seen.

## Reflection

What does it mean for you personally to abound in hope by the power of the Holy Spirit in your current circumstances? Where is hope most needed, and most difficult?

## Today's Practice

Pray Romans 15:13 as a blessing over your church today: 'May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, so that by the power of the Holy Spirit you may abound in hope.' Pray it by name over three people in your community.
