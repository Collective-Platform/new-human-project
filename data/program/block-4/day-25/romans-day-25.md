## Today's Focus

_A hundred days in, will we allow this gospel to remain mere ideas in our heads, or will we step out into the world and embody this Spirit filled, hope overflowing new humanity?_

## Reading Notes

Paul closes Romans with a song of praise that gathers everything together: to him who is able to strengthen you according to my gospel and the preaching of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery that was kept secret for long ages but has now been disclosed, to the only wise God be glory forevermore through Jesus Christ. The mystery is no longer hidden. It has been revealed in the gospel. And the purpose of that revelation is not merely individual salvation but the obedience of faith among all the nations. The New Human Project is not a personal spirituality programme. It is God's cosmic plan: to create a new humanity, drawn from every people, shaped by the gospel, indwelt by the Spirit, living as a sign and foretaste of the renewed world that is coming. You have now spent twenty five days in Romans, and before that in Ephesians, Philippians, and the Holy Spirit. You are part of this project. So is everyone in the room with you. The gospel is the power of God for salvation. And that power is still at work: in you, in your church, in the world.

## Key Idea

The New Human Project is not a church programme. It is God ancient, cosmic plan: now revealed in the gospel, carried by the Spirit, embodied in the new humanity, and moving toward the renewal of all things.

## Reflection

After twenty five days in Romans, and one hundred days in the New Human Project overall, what is the one truth that has most taken root in you? What do you want to carry forward? Name it, and speak it to someone.

## Today's Practice

Gather with your community. Read Romans 1:16 to 17 together, the beginning of the argument, and then Romans 16:25 to 27, the closing song of praise. Pray together: for your church, for your city, for the nations. And ask: what is our next step as the new humanity in this place?
