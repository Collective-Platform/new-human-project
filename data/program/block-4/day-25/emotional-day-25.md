**Scripture:** Psalm 139:23–24 \- **23** Search me, God, and know my heart; test me and know my anxious thoughts.**24** See if there is any offensive way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting.

Imagine your heart as a house. There are bright rooms filled with joy, gratitude, and memories of God's faithfulness.

But there may also be rooms you've locked. Perhaps one contains disappointment. Another holds grief. Another stores anger. Another hides unforgiveness.

Most of us naturally protect these rooms because they feel painful. Yet healing rarely happens behind locked doors.

David prayed one of the bravest prayers in Scripture: "Search me, O God... Know my heart." Notice he invited God to search—not because God didn't already know, but because David wanted to know himself.

**The Holy Spirit never exposes our hearts to embarrass us.** He reveals hidden places because He desires freedom for us. He is gentle. Patient. Never forceful.

**Transformation begins wherever honesty begins.**

Today, don't ask God to change everything overnight. Simply open one room. Invite Him inside.

That small act of surrender could become the beginning of lifelong healing.

## Practice

Take 5–10 minutes to sit quietly with God. Slowly read **Psalm 139:23–24** three times. Don't rush through the words. Allow them to become your prayer.

## Reflection

As you look back over the past 25 days, what has God been making you more aware of in your heart?
