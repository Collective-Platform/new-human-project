## MOVEMENT 4 — The Life We Are Called To

# The new humanity in practice — worship, community, and the world

Days 20–25 · Romans 12–16

---

Romans 12 begins with 'therefore', and that word carries the weight of eleven chapters. Because of everything God has done, justifying the ungodly, uniting Jew and Gentile, working all things together for his purposes, separating no one from his love, therefore, present your bodies as a living sacrifice. The final movement of Romans is not an appendix of practical tips. It is the outworking of the gospel in every dimension of life, in worship, in community, in the use of gifts, in relationships with outsiders and enemies, in submission to authority, in the welcome of the weak. The new humanity that God has been building across eleven chapters now has to be lived out, in Rome, in every city, in your church, today.
