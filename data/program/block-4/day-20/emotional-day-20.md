**Scripture:** Matthew 6:34 \- **34** Therefore do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will worry about itself. Each day has enough trouble of its own.

Most anxiety doesn't come from today's responsibilities.

It comes from tomorrow's possibilities. "What if I fail?" "What if something goes wrong?" "What if I don't have enough?"

Jesus understood our tendency to live ahead of ourselves. That is why He said, "Do not worry about tomorrow..." **Notice He didn't deny tomorrow's challenges.**

Instead, He reminded us that tomorrow will have its own grace.

One of the greatest invitations of Christian mindfulness is learning to return our attention to the present moment with God.

The Holy Spirit always meets us in the present.

We cannot experience tomorrow's grace today because we are not living there yet.

God gives daily bread. Daily strength. Daily mercy.

Not yearly supplies all at once. **Trust grows one day at a time.**

Instead of asking, "What if everything goes wrong?" perhaps ask, "What is God inviting me to trust Him with today?"

## Practice

List three things you are thankful for today.

## Reflection

How often do you imagine worst-case scenarios?
