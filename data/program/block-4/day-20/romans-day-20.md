## Today's Focus

_What does it mean to offer yourself as a living sacrifice and why is this the only reasonable response to Romans 1–11?_

## Reading Notes

“I appeal to you therefore, brothers, by the mercies of God, to present your bodies as a living sacrifice.” Paul's appeal is not to duty, not to fear, not to religious obligation. It is to mercy, the mercy that chapters 1 to 11 have described in full. In view of all that God has done, what is the reasonable response? To give yourself entirely to him. Not a dead sacrifice offered once, but a living sacrifice, a body that gets up every morning and is placed again at God's disposal. And the transformation required is specific: be transformed by the renewing of your mind, so that you can discern what is the will of God, what is good and acceptable and perfect. The new humanity is not conformed to the pattern of this age. Its mind is being renewed. Its values are being rewritten. It is learning to see the world through the categories of the gospel, and to live from that different vision.

## Key Idea

The whole of Romans 1 to 11 leads to this: present yourself, your body, your ordinary life, as a living sacrifice. This is not duty. It is the only logical response to mercies this great.

## Reflection

What would it look like for you to give your whole day to God tomorrow morning? What might you have to give up, and what freedom would you gain?

## Today's Practice

Begin tomorrow morning with a deliberate act of consecration: 'Lord, I present my body to you today, my hands, my words, my time, my attention. I am yours. Use me.' Make it the first thing you do.
