**Scripture:** Philippians 4:6–7 \- **6** Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God. **7** And the peace of God, which transcends all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus.

Worry has an interesting way of disguising itself. Sometimes it looks responsible. Sometimes it feels productive. Sometimes we convince ourselves that worrying shows how much we care.

But worry rarely changes tomorrow. It usually steals today's peace.

Paul tells believers: "Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation... present your requests to God." Notice that Paul doesn't tell us to ignore anxiety. Neither does he tell us to simply "stop worrying." Instead, he gives us a new direction.

Whenever anxiety rises, let prayer rise higher. Anxiety often comes from imagining countless future possibilities we cannot control.

Prayer brings us back to the God who is already present in every future we fear. The Holy Spirit continually reminds us that God is not surprised by tomorrow.

While we only see uncertainty, God sees the entire journey. Christian mindfulness teaches us to remain present with God instead of constantly living in imagined futures.

Every anxious thought can become an invitation to pray. Every worry can become an opportunity to trust.

**Peace is not the absence of uncertainty.** Peace is the presence of God in the middle of uncertainty.

## Practice

Write down every worry currently occupying your mind.
