We often think we need to become stronger before coming to God. We tell ourselves, "Once I feel better, then I'll pray." But Romans 8 reminds us that the Holy Spirit comes to us in our weakness, not after we've overcome it.

Notice that Paul doesn't say the Spirit helps us when we have everything together. He helps us **in our weakness**. That includes emotional exhaustion, anxiety, confusion, disappointment, and even moments when we don't know what to pray.

The first step towards emotional health isn't fixing yourself—it's becoming aware of where you truly are. God cannot heal the version of you that you pretend to be. He heals the real you.

Christian mindfulness isn't about emptying your mind; it's about becoming fully aware that God is present in this moment. Before you try to change your emotions, pause long enough to notice them and invite the Holy Spirit into them.

Awareness is where transformation begins.

## Practice

Pause for one minute

## Reflection

1. What emotion best describes today?
2. Have you been hiding any emotions from God?
