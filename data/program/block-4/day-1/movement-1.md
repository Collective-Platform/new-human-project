MOVEMENT 1 The Gospel We Need _Why the good news is better — and more urgent — than we think_ Days 1–7 • Romans 1–4

Romans begins not with comfort but with diagnosis. Paul is writing to a church in the capital of the world's most powerful empire, a city of culture, religion, law, and achievement. And his opening claim is staggering: every human being, without exception, stands before God in the same condition. Jew and Gentile, moral and immoral, religious and irreligious, all have fallen short. The good news of Romans cannot be heard properly until the bad news has been faced honestly. These seven days follow Paul's careful argument from the universality of human need to the breathtaking sufficiency of God's solution: righteousness given as a gift, received through faith, demonstrated at the cross.
