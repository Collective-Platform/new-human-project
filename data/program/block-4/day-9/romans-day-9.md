## Today's Focus

_How does Paul's contrast between Adam and Christ help us understand the depth of what Christ has done?_

## Reading Notes

Paul introduces a comparison that spans the whole of human history: Adam and Christ. Through one man’s act, sin entered the world and death spread to all. Yet through one man's act of righteousness, the free gift of justification came to all. Paul is not drawing a simple parallel, he is insisting on a contrast. If death spread through Adam's single act, how much more will grace and the free gift of righteousness reign in life through the one man Jesus Christ\! That phrase “how much more” is crucial. The gospel is not a restoration to Eden. It is something far greater. Christ does not simply undo the damage of Adam. He gives us something that was never in Eden at all: righteousness as a gift, grace that reigns, and eternal life in union with the Son of God. The new humanity does not start where Adam left off. It starts from a position of grace that far exceeds every deficit sin has ever created.

## Key Idea

Where sin increased, grace abounded all the more. The gospel does not merely undo what Adam did. It gives something far greater than Eden ever held.

## Reflection

Is there an area of your life where you feel the damage done by sin, by others, or by circumstance is too deep for grace to reach? How does Paul's promise in Romans 5:20 challenge that fear?

## Today's Practice

Write down the thing in your life or history that feels most beyond redemption. Then write Romans 5:20b next to it: “Where sin increased, grace abounded all the more.” Pray over that pairing.
