**Scripture:** Psalm 46:10 \- **10** He says, “Be still, and know that I am God; I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted in the earth.”

We live in constant noise. Notifications.

Meetings. Social media. Conversations.

Many of us are afraid of silence because silence forces us to face ourselves.

Yet Scripture repeatedly invites us to slow down.

"Be still, and know that I am God."

**Stillness isn't laziness. It's trust.**

When we slow down, we begin noticing God's presence, our emotions, and the pace we've been living.

The Holy Spirit often speaks most clearly when everything else becomes quiet.

## Practice

Spend three minutes in silence. No phone. No music. Just breathe and repeat: "Jesus, I am here."

## Reflection

What was difficult about slowing down?
