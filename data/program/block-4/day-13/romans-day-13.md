## Today's Focus

_What is the life in the Spirit that Romans 8 describes and how is it different from ordinary religious effort?_

## Reading Notes

‘There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus.’ This single sentence is one of the most liberating in Scripture. The verdict has been given. It cannot be revisited. And from that settled verdict flows a new quality of life: ‘the law of the Spirit of life has set you free from the law of sin and death.’ Paul describes a life lived not in the flesh, which is the self directed, sin controlled existence of chapter 7, but in the Spirit. ‘To set the mind on the Spirit is life and peace.’ ‘Those who are led by the Spirit of God are sons of God.’ And ‘the Spirit himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God.’ The new humanity is not a community of people trying harder to be good. It is a community of people who know, at the deepest level, that they are children of their Father, led by his Spirit, and that the verdict of the cross is final.

## Key Idea

No condemnation. Not reduced condemnation. Not condemnation depending on performance. No condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus. That is the foundation of life in the Spirit.

## Reflection

Do you live from a deep sense of “no condemnation”, or does a background sense of God’s disapproval shape the way you approach him? What would it take for you to truly receive this final verdict today?

## Today's Practice

Say Romans 8:1 out loud. Slowly, with your own name inserted: “There is therefore now no condemnation for \[name\], who is in Christ Jesus.” Say it three times. Then sit in what that means.
