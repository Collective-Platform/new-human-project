**Scripture:** Roman 12:15 \- **15** Rejoice with those who rejoice; mourn with those who mourn.

Many people can describe their schedule better than their emotions.

We say we're "busy," but underneath may be loneliness, fear, disappointment, or frustration. When emotions remain unnamed, they often begin controlling our behaviour without us realising it.

Romans 12 tells believers to rejoice with those who rejoice and mourn with those who mourn. That requires emotional awareness. You cannot process emotions you refuse to acknowledge.

Jesus never ignored emotions. He rejoiced, grieved, became angry at injustice, and wept openly. His emotions were never sinful; they simply revealed what mattered to His heart.

The Holy Spirit helps us honestly name our emotions—not so we become controlled by them, but so we can surrender them to God.

**Naming an emotion is often the first step towards healing.**
