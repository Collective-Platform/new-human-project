## MOVEMENT 3 — The Story We Are Part Of

# Israel, the Gentiles, and the faithfulness of God

Days 15–19 · Romans 9–11

---

Romans 9 to 11 is the section most readers skip, and most preachers avoid. It is also, on many readings, the theological heart of the entire letter. Paul is wrestling with the most urgent question raised by the gospel: if God made promises to Israel, and Israel has largely rejected her Messiah, has the word of God failed? The answer Paul gives is one of the most sophisticated and pastorally important in all of Scripture, and it ends not in a systematic conclusion but in pure worship. These five days slow down with Paul in these chapters. They ask what these verses mean for our understanding of God, of history, and of our own place in a story that is far larger than we imagined.
