**Scripture:** Romans 8:1 \- Therefore, there is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus, …

There is a difference between conviction and shame.

Conviction says, _"You made a mistake."_ Shame says, _"You are the mistake."_

One leads us towards Jesus. The other pushes us away from Him.

Many people carry shame long after God has already forgiven them. We remember old failures. Broken relationships. Poor decisions. Words we wish we could take back.

Over time, these memories quietly shape our identity.

But Romans 8 begins with a declaration that completely changes how we see ourselves: "**There is now no condemnation**..."

Condemnation is the constant voice that tells us we are beyond hope.

The Holy Spirit never speaks that language. Instead, He lovingly convicts us so that we can experience healing, repentance, and restoration.

**Your identity is not determined by your lowest moment**. It is determined by the finished work of Christ.

**Emotional healing often begins when we stop defining ourselves by what happened to us or what we have done**. Instead, we begin believing what God says about us.

**Grace does not ignore our failures. Grace transforms them.**

## Practice

Write one sentence that you often tell yourself.

## Reflection

Do you tend to live under conviction or condemnation?
