**Scripture:** Psalm 42:5 \- Why, my soul, are you downcast? Why so disturbed within me? Put your hope in God, for I will yet praise him, my Savior and my God.

God created us as body, soul, and spirit. Often, our emotions appear in our bodies before we recognise them in our minds.

Stress may become tight shoulders.

Fear becomes shallow breathing.

Anxiety keeps us awake.

Fatigue makes us impatient.

Our bodies are not our enemies—they're invitations to slow down and pay attention.

The psalmist repeatedly asked, "Why, my soul, are you downcast?" He noticed what was happening inside instead of ignoring it.

The Holy Spirit often uses our physical awareness to reveal deeper spiritual needs.

Today, **listen to your body with compassion** rather than criticism.

## Practice

Spend one minute scanning your body. Where do you feel tension? Invite Jesus into that place.

## Reflection

What is your body trying to tell you?
