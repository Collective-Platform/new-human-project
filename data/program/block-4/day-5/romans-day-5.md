## Today's Focus

_Why does Paul bring together so many Old Testament voices to make this one point?_

## Reading Notes

Paul reaches his conclusion with a linked chain of Old Testament quotations, all making the same point: there is no one righteous, not even one. He draws from Psalms, Ecclesiastes, and Isaiah, spanning the whole sweep of Scripture. The effect is deliberate: this is not Paul's opinion. It is the consistent testimony of Israel's own sacred texts. These Scriptures speak of mouths full of destruction, feet swift to shed blood, and a path of peace people do not know. And then comes the devastating summary: whatever the law says, it speaks to those under the law, so that every mouth may be silenced and the whole world held accountable to God. The purpose of the law is not salvation. It is a diagnosis. It silences every mouth, whether immoral, moral, or religious, and leaves the whole world without excuse before God. This is where every human being stands before the gospel arrives.

## Key Idea

The law cannot save us. It can only diagnose us. Its purpose is to silence all our excuses and reveal our true condition before God. That total honesty is where the gospel begins.

## Reflection

What does it feel like to stand completely silent before God, with all your excuses gone? How can facing this truth bring you peace instead of shame?

## Today's Practice

Read Romans 3:10–18 and pray those verses back to God as an honest confession. Pray about the human heart condition, not just individual sins. Spend a few quiet minutes resting in His presence before moving on to Day 6 tomorrow.
