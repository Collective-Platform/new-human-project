## Today's Focus

_Why does Abraham matter so much to Paul's argument about justification by faith?_

## Reading Notes

Paul's argument in chapter 4 is strategic and brilliant. If justification by faith were a brand new idea, we would have reason to doubt it. Instead, Paul demonstrates that it is the oldest truth in Scripture. Abraham was counted righteous before he was circumcised and long before the law existed, simply because he believed God. The faith God credited as righteousness was not impressive theological knowledge. It was the response of a man who faced the stark reality that his body was as good as dead, and his wife Sara's womb was dead, yet he grew strong in faith and gave glory to God. He was fully convinced that God was able to fulfill what He promised. Abraham was justified by trusting God's word against all human evidence. Paul stresses that this was written not for Abraham alone, but for us. The same God who raised Jesus from the dead asks us to trust Him with the same honest, unpretending faith. The foundation of the new humanity is not performance. It is trust.

## Key Idea

Abraham was justified not by what he did but by what he believed. The foundation of the new humanity, across every century is simply trusting God's word against all contrary evidence.

## Reflection

Where in your current circumstances do you need the same faith as Abraham? What impossible situation is God asking you to trust Him with today?

## Today's Practice

Read Romans 4:18–21 aloud, slowly. Then write down one area of your life where you are being asked to trust God against what the evidence suggests. Pray it back to him with Abraham's words: _“Fully convinced that God is able.”_
